# AI Overviews and Local Search Behavior

Goal: Understand how AI Overviews reshape local intent, visibility, and clicks.

## The Shift: What's Really Changing

**How AI Overviews appear:**
- AI Overviews now appear *above the map pack* at times, stealing clicks before users scroll
- Local intent queries ("roofers near me," "best plumber in DC") trigger AI summaries showing 3–5 local businesses
- Fewer clicks → more zero-click conversions (calls, directions, messages straight from SERP)
- Reviews and sentiment are being summarized, not just listed
- AI evaluates context, not just keywords (entity strength, trust signals, and topical authority now matter more)

## How AI Alters Local Intent

**Searcher behavior is changing:**
- Users ask questions, not keywords:  
  - "Who's the most trusted roofer near me?" vs "roofers Baltimore MD"
- AI interprets trust and authority from reviews, citations, and consistency
- Searcher behavior shifts from "browse and compare" → "trust and act"
- Local search becomes conversational — more about recommendations than rankings

## What Happens to Map Packs and Reviews

**Impact on traditional local rankings:**
- Map Packs shrink or move lower; AI Overview pre-filters which 2–3 listings appear
- Star ratings are less visible; review language and sentiment are quoted instead
- Volume still matters, but velocity (recent reviews) and context (mentions of service/location) carry extra weight
- Businesses with rich, structured data and review freshness dominate the AI layer

## How to Appear in AI-Summarized Local Results

**Core strategy:**
1. **Optimize GBP as a "data source"** not a brochure
2. **Build review velocity + sentiment**
   - Recent, descriptive, location-rich feedback
   - AI weights recency and specificity heavily
3. **Add LocalBusiness + Service schema** to all location pages
   - Help AI understand your entity and service areas
4. **Publish FAQ and proof content** AI can cite
   - Short, factual, scannable content that answers common questions
5. **Ensure NAP consistency** across every citation
   - Removes friction for AI matching your business across sources
6. **Strengthen entity connections**
   - Mentions in local news, directories, associations
   - Build topical authority in your service area

## Key Takeaway

In the AI Overviews era, SEO success requires moving beyond keyword rankings to *entity strength* — proving your business is trustworthy, consistent, and worth recommending based on real customer signals, structured data, and contextual relevance.
