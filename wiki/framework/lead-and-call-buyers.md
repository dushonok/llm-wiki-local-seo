# Lead & Call Buyers (Pay-Per-Call Networks)

**Purpose:** Where to sell calls/leads from a microsite when there's no local client
renting it yet (or alongside one). The alternative to selling direct to a business per
lead — see [[pricing-and-deal-structures.md]] Play #2 — and the exit path for a
rank-and-rent site that ranks but hasn't been rented.

**How it works:** the network gives you a tracking number per offer; a call routed
through it pays out if it meets the offer's rules (minimum duration, caller in the
service area, real service need). Buyers can dispute calls for a few days — disputed
calls aren't paid.

## Finding offers: OfferVault 🏘️
https://offervault.com/?selectedTab=topOffers&search=&page=1 — shared in the community.
An affiliate-offer search engine (since 2009) that lists offers across many networks, so you can
compare networks for one niche in one place instead of signing up everywhere first.
- **How to use it:** filter Category = *Pay Per Call* (+ *Home Services*), Country = *United States*
  (or Canada), then search the niche (`mold`, `plumbing`, `sewer`, `septic`, `locksmith`, `radon`).
  Each row shows payout, countries and network; open the offer for call duration (buffer),
  hours, geo and **"Traffic Accepted"** — confirm it lists **SEO** (and GMB if the number will
  be on a GBP).
- **Reading payouts:** `$0.00` usually means RTB/dynamic pricing (the buyer bids per call), not
  a zero payout — e.g. Marketcall's mold remediation and roofing bundles show $0.00.
- **Use it to vet networks:** network profiles show acceptance rules (eLocal: experienced
  affiliates only) and OfferVault's Top Networks Survey (Lead Smart has placed top 3 every year since 2020).
- **Caution:** it's a directory, not a buyer — terms, payouts and acceptance are set by each
  network; check the offer in the network's own dashboard before building around it. Whether
  browsing needs an OfferVault account wasn't confirmed.

## Rules of thumb
- **Check payout coverage before building.** Lead Smart's and CallScaler's public
  payout-by-ZIP pages show whether a niche+town has a buyer and what it pays — fold this
  into niche selection ([[niche-selection-strategy.md]]).
- **Canada (Ontario sites):** most networks are US-focused; Canadian demand is thin and
  offer-by-offer (e.g. Lead Smart's limited Canada test). Selling direct to a local
  business is still the main path there — see [[sales-and-lead-generation.md]].
- **Quality decides payout.** Wrong-number, out-of-area and "service not offered" calls
  get disputed and can get you dropped (Service Direct, Client Connector). Make the site
  say clearly what's offered and where.
- **TCPA compliance** is required by these networks — organic inbound calls to a number
  on your site are the cleanest source.
- Networks pay less per call than a direct client, but no chasing payments — the
  per-lead non-payment risk in [[pricing-and-deal-structures.md]] Play #2 goes away.
- Unverified: exact niche coverage for septic inspection, sewer line repair, radon and
  car detailing — check each network's offer list after signing up.
