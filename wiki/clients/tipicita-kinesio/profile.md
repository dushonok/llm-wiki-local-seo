## ⚠️ Naming nuance (important for keyword work)

"Kinésiologue" ≠ "kinésithérapeute" (physio) in French. Never target or bid on
physiotherapy-intent keywords for this client — the domain name itself
(`tipicita-kinesio.com`) risks attracting confused search traffic. Keyword and
content work should stay in the kinésiologie / coaching de vie / bien-être
émotionnel lane.
