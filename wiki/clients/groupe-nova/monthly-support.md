# Ongoing monthly support after the audit — groupenova.ca

Proposal for the retainer that follows the fixed-price audit project (see
[4-week-plan.md](4-week-plan.md)). Pending: the client's answer on service-area
pages (see "Open question for the client").

## Pricing structure

- **$600/month for the first 3 months** (about 17.1h/month, 51.4h total): clears
  the post-audit backlog and gets the site ready for the Google Ads launch.
- **$300/month ongoing** (about 8.6h/month) after that.
- Rate: **$35/h**, from the owner's estimates sheet. Because the fee is flat, hours
  can float between months as long as the 3-month total stays at about 51h.
- Dates assume the audit finishes in November, so the 3 months are December,
  January and February, with ads launching February–March 2027.

## What the larger items cover

### Local landing page (SEO)

A page built to rank for one service in one area, such as a French page for kitchen
renovation in Laval. It leads with the service that has the best proof in that area
and briefly links to the other three.

- **Area + main service:** target phrase in the URL, title, H1, meta description
  and opening paragraph; the service pages, GBP and footer link to it.
- **Project proof:** 2–3 real projects with before/after photos, a short
  description, the town named in captions and alt text, and a local review if
  possible. Proof exists for Laval and Brossard; there is none yet for Lanaudière,
  the North Shore or the South Shore, so the client must send projects or the page
  must say plainly that it serves the area.
- **FAQ:** 4–6 real questions (timeline, permits, quotes, budget range) from the
  client's practice.
- **Schema:** `Service`, `areaServed` and `BreadcrumbList`, building on the
  sitewide business markup (Rec #10). FAQ markup is allowed but rarely produces
  rich results now.
- **Risks:** doorway pages (near-identical pages with a town name swapped; each
  needs its own proof, FAQ and local detail, or hold the page back), a fake address
  (use "serving" wording and keep the Rue Saint-Denis address only), and
  cannibalisation with the service page (service page for the general service,
  area page for service + area).
- The earlier 5h-per-page estimate broke down as 3.5h FR (research and outline
  0.5, writing 1.25, Elementor build 1, captions, alt text, title, links and
  schema 0.5, quality check 0.25) and 1.5h EN. The sheet uses 1.5h total.

### Homepage optimized for Montreal

Montreal is the main market. The homepage title, meta description, schema and alt
text are already covered in the audit; this item is the visible page: H1 with
service and Montréal, Montreal intro copy, links to the 4 service pages, a
service-area section linking to the location pages, project proof with captions,
a short FAQ, and the form. Sheet estimate 1.5h (the assistant leans towards 2h
since it is the highest-stakes page).

### GBP photo and Q&A additions (1.5h)

Backlog from Rec #4 ([recommendations.md](recommendations.md)): 20+ real project
photos and pre-filled questions.

- Photos (about 1h): pick 20–25 from the client's existing material (site
  before/after galleries), prepare (descriptive file names, resize, no personal
  details), upload to the correct categories, set cover and logo, check on mobile.
- Q&A (about 0.5h): 6–8 questions a homeowner asks (timeline, areas, free quotes,
  languages, permits and RBQ licence, project types), French first with short
  English where useful.
- Q&A is posted from the public listing in Google Maps or Search (section
  "Questions & answers"), signed in with the managing account, not from the
  dashboard. Google has been changing and reducing this feature, so check the live
  listing first. If it is missing, put the same content in the Services section
  (short description per service and areas), the business description (750
  characters), and FAQ blocks on the website. The 0.5h stays the same.
- Not included: collecting the photos from the client, new photography, and
  permission to show clients' homes.

### Google Ads landing pages (separate from the website)

- Separate from the normal pages: no menu or footer links, one goal (form or call),
  heading that matches the ad, form above the fold, short and fast.
- Hosted on the same domain (for example `/promo/`) with a blank Elementor Canvas
  layout; set to `noindex` and kept out of the sitemap, not blocked in robots.txt
  (ad reviewers must read them).
- Trust signals: RBQ, CCQ and APCHQ badges, the 5.0 rating with the real 11
  reviews, 2–3 project photos, short process, tap-to-call button on mobile.
- Thank-you page fires the conversion; privacy notice linked under the form
  (Quebec Law 25), and a cookie consent banner may be needed (not yet checked).
- One page per region (6 pages) with a service picker in the form, rather than one
  page per service per region (24). Add service-specific pages later if the data
  shows a need. Extra pages cost about 1h each.
- Ad phone calls can use a Google forwarding number that only appears in the ad,
  so it does not clash with the consistent business phone number elsewhere.

## How the Google Ads launch (February–March 2027) affects the plan

- Deadline: everything the ads send people to must be live and tracked by about
  February 1, so the ad pages are finished by January 31 for tracking tests and ad
  approval.
- The ad pages are separate from the SEO pages, so the SEO location pages are not
  needed before launch and move to February.
- Tracking and the baseline are mandatory before launch, so the ads can be
  compared against organic results.
- The monthly report separates organic and Google Business Profile results from
  paid ones (+1h per month while ads run).
- Ads do not improve organic rankings; the search-terms data they produce replaces
  the keyword guesses in [keywords.md](keywords.md) and should drive the blog
  topics after March.

## Embedded forms instead of CTA buttons (owner's addition)

Usually lifts quote requests on service pages because the visitor does not leave
the page. Points to handle:

1. **Fix the phone-field problem first.** The site's contact form uses a
   country-code phone plugin whose country list loading expanded is what breaks
   `/en/services-2/interior-design-services/`
   ([interior-design-page-bug-confirmed-2026-10-19.md](../../../raw/clients/groupe-nova/interior-design-page-bug-confirmed-2026-10-19.md)).
   Putting that form on every page would repeat the bug; the fix is in the audit's
   week 1.
2. Keep the form short (name, phone or email, service, area, message) and keep a
   tap-to-call button on mobile.
3. Track submissions by page and add spam protection.
4. Add a privacy notice link under the form (Quebec Law 25; confirm wording).
5. Traffic is too low for a formal A/B test; compare quote requests per month
   before and after.

## Open question for the client

Client service areas as stated: Montreal, Montreal East, West Island, North Shore,
South Shore and Lanaudière. The original brief listed Montreal, Laval, North Shore,
South Shore, Montérégie and Lanaudière, and real project photos exist for Laval and
Brossard (Montérégie). Planning assumption: one page per region, 6 ad pages and
5 SEO location pages (Montreal is covered by the homepage), with Laval and
Montérégie covered inside the North Shore and South Shore pages. This is
unconfirmed.

Text to send:

"Which service areas do you want dedicated pages for? We understand your areas are Montreal, Montreal East, West Island, North Shore, South Shore and Lanaudière, and we are planning one page per region. Please confirm this list, and tell us whether Laval and the Montérégie should have their own pages or be covered inside the North Shore and South Shore pages. Also tell us whether you plan to run Google Ads in every region or only some of them, so we know how many ad pages to build. We need your answer by early December so the pages are ready for a February launch."

If the region list changes: each ad page adds about 1h; each SEO location page adds
1.5h plus 0.5h of forms.

## Honest limits

- Rankings and calls take about 3–6 months to move; the report should show
  direction, not promise positions.
- "Calls generated through Google" is only partly measurable: GBP shows call-button
  taps, direction requests and website clicks; counting real organic phone calls
  needs a call-tracking number, which must be added to the GBP as a secondary number
  so the main number stays consistent everywhere (see
  [recommendations.md](recommendations.md) #2 on the old-number problem).
- Content quality depends on real project photos and details from the client
  (project types, areas, price ranges).
