The Refinishing Process
We take care of your home as if it were our own and go the extra mile to make sure your space is safe and protected throughout the entire reglazing process. Our refinishers use the best low VOC commercial-grade reglazing compounds that are only available to professionals, so your refinished surfaces will look great for years. Delivering beautiful results is a point of pride for us, but more than anything, we want you to be happy with your reglazing and work hard to earn client trust and referrals.
Preparation

We remove caulk and paint and use industrial cleaners to lift oils and soap scum. After setting up proper ventilation, we sand and smooth damaged areas, applying high-strength filler to fix chips. Next, we sand the entire surface to help the new glaze adhere better. After a final degreasing, we mask off hardware and surrounding areas to protect them from overspray.

Application
Our professional spray guns give a beautifully smooth finish. Reglazing usually takes 24 hours to dry fully. Cold weather can extend the curing time up to 36 hours. We make sure there are no drip marks or uneven patches and everything looks perfect.
Aftercare
Enjoy your new space and use our pro tips to keep it looking pristine for years. Avoid using abrasive cleansers and scrubbing pads, don’t use harsh chemicals like bleach or acids, and use a soft squeegee or cloth after use to prevent soap scum and mineral deposit buildup.
FAQs
Our most commonly asked questions about reglazing.
How much does reglazing cost in Long Island?

Depending on the type of material we’re working with and the size and condition of your fixture, you’ll probably pay on average between $350 – $1,000 for various reglazing services. This is a great alternative to replacing your fixtures and much less expensive.

Are you a local Long Island reglazing contractor?

Yes we are! We offer refinishing services to the entire island, including neighborhoods in the North and South Shore areas, and throughout Nassau, and Suffolk Counties.

How long does it take for reglazed surfaces to dry?

In the summer months, especially if you’re close to the shores, it only takes 24 hours for your refinished surface to fully cure, thanks to Long Island’s humidity and warm weather. In winter months when it’s colder, it’s normal for cure times to take about 36 hours.

How does your reglazing company in Long Island make sure the glaze will last longer than other companies?

Our reglazing experts are experienced, and well-trained and use premium-grade reglazing compound that’s made specifically for residential clients looking for a long-lasting finish–even our commercial clients love the results.

Can I change the color of my tub, sink, or tile?

Yes you can! We get this question all the time, especially since many Long Island homes were built 20 or 30+ years ago. Reglazing is a popular option if you want to modernize and update the look and color of your bathroom.
