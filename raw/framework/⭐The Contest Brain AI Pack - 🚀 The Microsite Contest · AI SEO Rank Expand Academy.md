⭐The Contest Brain: AI Pack

**TLDR:** Everything we learned from the Microsite Contest, packed into one zip file you can give to an AI assistant. It reads the evidence and helps you make decisions about your own microsite. The file is attached to this page.

## What this is

The Contest Brain is the full record of the contest in a form an AI can read. Every ranking we recorded. A teardown of the two sites that reached number one. The winner’s own account and his working records. Every study and every daily update.

You load it into ChatGPT, Claude or any assistant that takes files. Then you ask it questions about your own site.

## Do these first

This is the third step, not the first.

Go through the Microsite Playbook modules. Then go through the Microsite Empire module, which gives you the Microsite Blueprint. Then use this.

The Playbook and the Blueprint teach you how to build. The Brain does not repeat them. It is the evidence of what worked when 61 sites were built and measured. The brain is what will allow you to stay up to date with SEO tactics because we base on it real data extracted recently.

## How to use it

**The quick way.** Download the file below, upload it to your LLM of choice (Claude Code, GPT, Grok, whatever)

## What to ask it (examples)

Is this service and town worth building in?

My site dropped. What do I do?

How many listings do I need, and which ones?

Should I start a blog?

Here is my site. What would the contest say is missing?

Plan my first 30 days the way the winner ran his.

Compare my current site to this brain...is there any improvements I should be doing?

## What it is firm about

The Brain holds 60 numbered facts and 15 rules. Every fact is marked as something we measured, something counted from the sites, or something the winner told us. When it gives you advice, it tells you which facts it is using.

Three examples. The winner reached number one with 47 pages. He built 20 to 25 listings in his first days and 5 to 10 every week after that. A typical scoring site had about 3,750 words on its home page, and a typical site that never scored had about 2,100.

## What is inside

The three core files are about 7,400 words, so they fit in any chat.

Behind them are digests of both number one sites, the winner’s account, his full list of 63 listings, his 82 social posts and his links. There are 13 data files with every ranking, 35 daily updates, every handout as a PDF and as plain text, and the search link tool we used for every check.

If you want the winner’s part as something to read and print, that is [The Winner’s Playbook](https://www.skool.com/rank-expand-academy/classroom/7f75a163?md=7554407690b04bb1bd2d946cd91930dd&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=e0c04038afe74a28a97dccc4b03256df "https://www.skool.com/rank-expand-academy/classroom/7f75a163?md=7554407690b04bb1bd2d946cd91930dd&amp;utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&amp;utm_content=e0c04038afe74a28a97dccc4b03256df").

## Three things to know

**It is frozen.** Rankings stop at the final check on November 17, 2026.

**The winning site is there to study, not to copy.** Use the structure. Write your own words.

**The radon number one is described, not copied.** It was never entered in the contest, so there is no copy of it in the file.

This file is for Academy members. Please do not share it outside the community.
