Microsite Contest Day 22: One Contestant Is Beating Everyone (on both queries)

Today is a fun update.

⭐Where things stand

- One contestant is scoring. Mold site at 10, radon site at 22. 12 points.
- One contestant is close on both. Mold at 22, radon at 34. Zero points.
- About eight contestants have a mold site somewhere between 24 and 35 and nothing on radon.
- The contestant buying press releases has three of them on radon page 1. Their own sites are at 46 and out of the top 50.
- The person who led mold for the first two weeks is out of the top 50 on mold and at 39 on radon.
- Everyone else, roughly 20 contestants, has nothing in the top 50 on either query.

⭐Why the leader is winning

I've watched both of this contestant's sites every day for three weeks. It comes down to four things.

They built the same site twice. Same template, same schema, same setup on mold and on radon.

They made the business look real everywhere. One name, one address, one phone number, copied onto about 15 profiles. Facebook, YouTube, Houzz, Clutch, Manta, Crunchbase, Pinterest, and more.

All of them listed in the site's code. Google sees the same business, described the same way, on 15 sites it already trusts.

Those profiles rank around the site.

On mold: Facebook at 6, the site at 10, MapQuest at 11, Clutch at 12. On radon: Houzz at 8, Crunchbase at 17, the site at 22.

Nobody else has more than two results on either query.

This contestant has nine.

The homepage ranks, and it doesn't move.

Their homepage has held 10 to 13 every day this week.

Other contestants' sites swap between FAQ, contact and township pages daily, and their rankings swing with it. This site has one deep homepage that covers every service, and Google picks it every time.

⭐What they didn't do

No press. No daily republishing. No 100 page site.

⭐What they've done = fifty(ish) pages, built once, surrounded by profiles, left alone.

⭐The takeaway

A fresh domain can't hold a ranking by itself. The one site that's scoring is propped up by 15 listings that all say the same thing. Build the site, build the listings, match them exactly, then stop touching it.

⭐Scoreboard

- Mold: 1 site scoring, 12 points, best position 10
- Radon: 0 sites scoring, best position 22
