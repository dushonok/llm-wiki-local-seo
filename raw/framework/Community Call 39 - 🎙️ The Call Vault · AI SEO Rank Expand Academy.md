## **Also discussed 💬**

1. 📰 **Google AI Overviews data is coming to Search Console** — AIO + AI Mode data is in beta now (rolling out to some accounts). One to watch, though there's a catch on what it actually surfaces.
2. 🤔 **Schema & llms.txt for LLMs** — does structured data help you get cited in ChatGPT/Claude? Verdict leaned: it still helps comprehension even if citation weight is low. And review text with target keywords is clearly still moving Maps rankings.
3. 📊 **LLMs as a traffic channel** — split room. Some calling it the biggest growth channel right now, others seeing a handful of visitors a month. Jury's out — keep the content fresh either way.
4. 🌐 **SEO is an ecosystem play now** — we're selling GBP + site + CRM + content, not "a website." Marketing is everyone's job, not a siloed department.
5. 📞 **The "just a human" advantage** — as inboxes flood with AI pitches, personal calls and honest "not AI" outreach are converting.
6. ⚖️ **What it takes to sign and service a $15k/mo client** — often the same delivery as a $1k client, just more meetings. A whale raises your ceiling but concentrates risk, so stacking mid-tier clients can be the safer play.
7. 🛠️ **Tooling tips** — Data for SEO wired straight into Replit's Secrets tab for instant keyword/competitor research, and Local Falcon for spotting geo
