#51 - SEO Is Now Prompts and Agents

1:00:00

Jesse was at the beach, so Shawn ran a smaller, looser call. It opened on the emotional cycle of change, moved through a hard-niche reality check, and landed on where a lot of this is heading: rebuilding your whole toolstack as AI agents.

## Things mentioned

- Hermes ([https://hermes-agent.org](https://hermes-agent.org/ "https://hermes-agent.org")), the AI-agent platform Justin rebuilt his SEO toolstack inside (pick any model per task, self-learning, backs up to GitHub).
- OpenRouter ([https://openrouter.ai/](https://openrouter.ai/ "https://openrouter.ai/")), the "grocery store of models" behind it, most tasks on free models.
- Hostinger, which has a one-click Hermes install for a VPS.
- Index Bolt ([https://www.indexbolt.com/](https://www.indexbolt.com/ "https://www.indexbolt.com/")) and Omega Indexer, for indexing (Index Bolt is cheaper and faster now); Majestic Million ([https://majestic.com/reports/majestic-million](https://majestic.com/reports/majestic-million "https://majestic.com/reports/majestic-million")), for a trusted-domain check; Search Atlas ([https://searchatlas.com/](https://searchatlas.com/ "https://searchatlas.com/")).
- OpenArt AI ([https://openart.ai/suite/character](https://openart.ai/suite/character "https://openart.ai/suite/character")), which connects into GoHighLevel for avatar videos and social automation; [SomePoster.ai](http://someposter.ai/ "http://SomePoster.ai") ([https://someposter.ai/](https://someposter.ai/ "https://someposter.ai/")) for social posting.
- Astro ([https://astro.build/](https://astro.build/ "https://astro.build/")) with Claude Code and Replit ([https://replit.com/](https://replit.com/ "https://replit.com/")), the build-and-host stack for local sites.
- Cloudflare Pages, the free static host.
- SamCart, for the order-bump and upsell checkout.
- Porkbun and Namecheap, and [us.com](http://us.com/ "http://us.com"), for buying subdomains on high-authority domains.
- Stan Barry on YouTube, for GHL infographic and carousel prompts.

## What we covered

The call opened on a post from Ruth about the emotional cycle of change. You start something new full of optimism, hit the part where it feels like too much work, and most people quit right there and jump to the next shiny thing, resetting at the bottom every time. The people who get anywhere just keep doing the thing.

A member asked for feedback on a clean landing page he built for a done-for-you digital-product service. The room was honest that selling digital products is a savagely saturated space, first-page Reddit and Medium and Skillshare, so the move is not to rank it but to funnel it: go much further down the niche ladder to one specific need, write to one specific person, and push cheap traffic. There was a good aside that ugly sites convert, because real photos and real imperfections read as human where an AI-perfect site does not.

Justin walked through the segment that stuck. He rebuilt his whole SEO toolstack inside Hermes in about a week, an agent platform that lets you pick any model per task through OpenRouter and keeps learning instead of starting over every chat. One agent takes a backlink list, checks every link is alive, checks it against a trusted-domain list, checks whether Google already indexed it so he does not waste credits, then indexes the rest through Index Bolt and Omega automatically. His reframe, from a call with Shlomi, is that SEO now is less about ranking and more about building prompts and managing agents.

Bryan brought two things. A social-media offer built on OpenArt and GoHighLevel that needs no website or rankings, 30 days free then $500 a month, and a local-SEO win that proves the basics still work: his first Upwork client ranked with no backlinks at all, just a clean rebuild with the site semantically aligned, the schema in place, and everything interlinked, and impressions went from five thousand a day to twenty three thousand.

We closed on two notes. A domain-authority discussion that landed on links being an ingredient, not the cake, and Shawn teasing a backlink book coming soon, 850 vetted sources the team built themselves, time-unlocked for members.
