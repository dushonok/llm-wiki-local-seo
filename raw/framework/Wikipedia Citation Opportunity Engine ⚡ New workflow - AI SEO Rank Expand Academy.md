Wikipedia Citation Opportunity Engine ⚡ New workflow

Backlinks from Wikipedia are some of the most valuable links you can get, and almost nobody goes after them on purpose. This new workflow shows you exactly where the openings are.

👉 [#🆕 Wikipedia Citation Opportunity Engine](/rank-expand-academy/classroom/cf75b5d7?md=47a323385a924283b01bd89baca1a5e9)

Here's how it works. You give it a source URL. It fetches the page, pulls out the entities, and matches them to the Wikipedia pages closest to your topic. Then it finds the "citation needed" gaps on those pages and hands you a target list:

◆ The exact Wikipedia page and the paragraph with the gap

◆ The "citation needed" snippet you would be filling

◆ What to publish, with a suggested page focus and a content outline

◆ Why it fits, so you know the angle before you write a word

Those "citation needed" tags are real content gaps Wikipedia editors are asking someone to fill. Publish something that genuinely fills one, and if your site has the authority, you stand a real chance of earning the link. One tip from the walkthrough: the more niche and specialized you go, the bigger the opportunity.

A great way to build links for clients or microsites in a way that almost nobody else is doing.
