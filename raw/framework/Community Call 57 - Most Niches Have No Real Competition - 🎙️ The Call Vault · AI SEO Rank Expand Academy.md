## What we covered

The call opened on credit burn. One member's VAs keep hitting their daily Claude limits on a large content project. Suggestions were to check which models the VAs run, try Karpathy's [CLAUDE.md](http://claude.md/ "http://CLAUDE.md") rules, and drive Replit from Claude Code in the browser instead of paying for Replit's own credits.

Shawn showed his directory build: about $10 to scrape logos, [fal.ai](http://fal.ai/ "http://fal.ai") to test about 10 image models, then nano banana pro images across all 1,556 listings in about two hours for $245. The whole build is now under $350. There was no full review, just a 20 image sample, and images with tools in them are where the model still struggles.

New member James, a tattoo artist, showed his first directory and about 12 microsites. Feedback covered a Facebook pixel for retargeting, matching page names to how people search ("tattoos in Ridgewood"), and his Cloudflare numbers, which look like bot traffic he can filter by country.

On the radon contest, Blair's three parasite sites have brought in six leads and he sold one for $125. Members shared what they're automating with Claude in the browser: a full Google Site, an AdWords campaign, and a Loom to custom email follow-up sequence. Blair's agency is about to hit 150 clients from cold calling, and Shlomi has two $2,500 a month trial clients.

John, in SEO since 2012 and now going into rank and rent, explained tiered linking with GSA. The group's caveat: most local clients don't need backlinks. You'll know you need them when nothing else moves the needle, and for real clients the safer routes are paid guest posts or the Rolodex.
