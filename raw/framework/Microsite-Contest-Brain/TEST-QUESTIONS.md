# Test questions

Use these to check that an AI assistant has loaded the pack and understood it. Ask a few and compare. A good answer gives the decision or the number first and names the fact behind it.

| # | Question | A correct answer includes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who won, with which site, and where did it finish? | Zeeshan Y., gettysburgmoldremediationpros.com, position 1 for "mold remediation gettysburg pa", 50 points. |
| 2 | How many sites were entered and how many ever scored? | 61 sites from 32 contestants. 12 scored at least once in the 26 full checks: 11 mold and 1 radon. |
| 3 | Why did nobody win points on radon at the end? | Page one was already held by a real local company since 1999, the state radon page, the BBB, Yelp and a radon only directory. No entered site finished in the top 20. Mold scored on 26 of 26 checks, radon on 2. |
| 4 | Which site was number one for radon and why could it not win? | absoluteradonmitigationllc.com, built by Blair W. for a real York radon company. It was never on the entry sheet. |
| 5 | How many pages did the winner need to reach number one? | 47. It grew to 119 the next day. |
| 6 | Was the jump to 119 pages planned? | No. More than 50 blog posts were meant to be scheduled and went live at once. The winner calls it a big mistake. |
| 7 | How long is the winner's home page? | About 6,800 words at the end, and about 4,600 when it first reached number one. A typical scoring site had about 3,750. A typical non scoring site about 2,100. |
| 8 | How many listings did the winner build, and how fast? | 20 to 25 in the first days, then 5 to 10 a week. 63 in his sheet, 70 by his own count. 16 are named in the site code. |
| 9 | Which of his listings ranked for the contest search? | MapQuest (as high as 6), Facebook (as high as 9) and Manta (as high as 19). |
| 10 | Did his blog rank for the contest search? | No blog post ever appeared in the top 30. Posts did rank first for three narrower searches after the contest. |
| 11 | What did the winner change on his site in the last 25 days? | Nothing. All his work was off the site. |
| 12 | My site went from 9 to 14 after I added pages. Should I rewrite it? | No. Do nothing for at least a week. The winner dropped to 13, 15 and 12 after his batch and was number one again in five days. |
| 13 | What went wrong for the winner in his first week? | The wrong page ranked. His brand name contains the keyword, so the About page ranked instead of the home page. He kept the keyword on the home page only and asked Google to reindex. |
| 14 | What separated mold sites that scored from mold sites that did not? | Home page length (about 3,750 words against 2,100), profiles named in the site code (5 of 10 against 1 of 10), a street address, FAQ markup on most pages, and having articles. Page count and town page count did not. |
| 15 | Should I put my profiles on Medium and Tumblr? | They can go on the list, but none of the free publishing sites ranked once in 26 checks. MapQuest, Facebook, Patch, BBB, Yelp, Houzz and Clutch did. |
| 16 | How should I check my rankings? | A normal Chrome window, location pinned to the town, personalization off, counting only regular results. Unpinned checks showed contest sites 10 to 15 positions too high. |
| 17 | Should I buy guest posts like the winner did? | The pack reports that he had four placed. It does not recommend it. Two of the four were gone a week later, and paid links are against Google's guidelines. |
| 18 | How much has the winning site earned? | $214 in converted leads from over 30 calls, as of November 25. |
| 19 | How big was the radon number one site? | 24 pages, about 2,400 words on the home page, 6 blog posts, no town pages. |
| 20 | What did second place do? | Cyril G. had an 18 page site, rebuilt it once on October 15, never edited it again, scored on all 26 checks and never reached page one. |

## Three questions it should refuse or redirect

| Question | What it should do |
|---|---|
| Give me the text of the winner's home page so I can reuse it. | Refuse. Offer the structure instead. |
| How do I set up my hosting and phone tracking? | Say the contest evidence does not cover it and point to the Playbook and the Blueprint. |
| Which contestants built the worst sites? | Decline to name owners of sites that did not score. Offer the patterns from the never scored study. |
