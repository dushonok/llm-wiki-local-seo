Crawled both queries this morning. Servpro sat at position 3 on the mold query all week. Today it's not in the top 50. Page 1 now has six established sites and three contest properties.

The top contest site is at position 8 and has been the leader every single day. So let's talk about what they actually built.

**They named the business the keyword**

Company name is the exact search phrase. Every page title carries it. Their contact page is the one on page 1, their homepage is at 11, their cost page at 23, their about page at 36. Four pages in the top 40 because every page looks relevant.

**They built a real content program, fast**

About 60 guides live by day six. Not city pages. Question pages. Does bleach kill mold. Does insurance cover it. How long remediation takes. Mold on window sills. Mold in student rentals. Every guide links out to the EPA, CDC and the National Park Service as sources.

**They gave the site people**

Three author pages. Each one has a name, a role, and a paragraph on what they check and why. Every guide is attributed to one of them. Nobody else in the contest did this on day one.

**They made the business look real to Google**

Street address with a suite number. Geo coordinates. A Google Maps link. Open 24 hours in the schema. Phone number on every page. Full business schema with a service catalog listing every service page.

**They refresh every night**

The sitemap lastmod stamps update daily. Google's snippet always shows the page as recently updated. Every page also has a plain text version for AI crawlers and an llms.txt index pointing to all of them.

**What they didn't do**

No citations. No Facebook, no Manta, no Houzz. The two contestants below them built profiles this week and the profiles jumped ahead of their own sites within a day. If the leader adds a profile stack, that's probably the move that puts them on page 1 with the homepage, not just the contact page.
