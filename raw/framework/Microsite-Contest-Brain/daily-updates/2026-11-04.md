**How these rankings are measured**

Same method since Sept 30, same seven loads. A normal Chrome window with English and US set, personalization turned off in the URL, and a location parameter that places the search in Gettysburg or York. Three pages for mold, four for radon, ten seconds apart. I don't record a result until the footer shows the town name and "Results are not personalized." Organic blue links only, so ads, the map pack, the AI Overview and the forum block don't count. One position per domain, its best URL. Positions 1 through 20 score. When this post says number one, it means the first organic website on page one for the exact contest query from the contest town.

**What to take away**

- Both queries now have a brand new domain at number one. One is a contestant's, one isn't, and they got there the same way: profiles and press first, a site that matched them, one batch, then silence. That's the result of this experiment with two weeks to go, and it's the same answer on both queries.
- The pa .com crept from 22 to 18 with no shipment. The Patch pair popped to 20 and left. Slow movement sticks more often than jumps do. If you're between 20 and 26, you're not out of it, you're in the queue.
- Fourteen days. Anything shipped after this week won't be measured. The last useful move is the one that's been useful all along: make sure ten places on the web agree on who you are, then stop.
