**Two things I'm watching**

First, both sites that republished this week dropped afterward. One republished on the 1st, jumped to 10, and is at 21 today. The other redeployed on the 3rd and is gone. Two sites is not proof of anything. But the site sitting at 10 is the one that hasn't visibly changed in a week. If you're tempted to keep tweaking and redeploying, maybe let it sit.

Second, the honeymoon may be ending. Fresh sites often get a few weeks of generous rankings, then Google settles them where they belong. We're three weeks in and the board is thinning out. The sites still standing have one thing in common: something off the site pointing back at it.
