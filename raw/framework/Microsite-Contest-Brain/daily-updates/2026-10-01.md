**Day 17: Someone Started Spending Money**

Two things today. A measurement fix, and the first paid tactic of the contest.

**First, the fix**

I've been checking rankings without pinning the search location. This week I started running them located in Gettysburg and York with personalization off, the way a local customer would see them.

It changes the numbers a lot. Real local companies rank higher from inside the town, and contest sites sit 10 to 15 spots lower than I was reporting. So the scoreboards I posted earlier this week were too generous. "A contest homepage hit page 1" and "9 sites scoring" were wrong from a local searcher's view. From here on, every number is location pinned.

**Mold, as seen from Gettysburg**

- 2 sites scoring, 12 points
- Best contest site is at 12. Second is at 18. Then nothing until 22.
- Page 1 is all established companies, plus one contestant's Facebook page at 6

The leader is the site with the biggest citation stack. It holds three of the top 12 results: Facebook at 6, a Clutch profile at 11, and the site itself at 12. Second place is the 107 page township grid.

Biggest mover is a site that expanded to over 100 pages and put prices on its homepage. Up 17 spots in a day.

One contestant started posting one minute YouTube videos with the town name in the title. Two of them are already in the top 50.

**Radon, as seen from York**

Zero points. Best contest site is at 32.

But this is the interesting part. A contestant running a directory site paid for a press release yesterday. It ranked on page 1 at position 7 within 17 hours.

The angle is smart. The release isn't about the contestant. It's about a real local radon company being featured in their directory. It links to the directory's profile page for that company. It's their second release in a week.

The directory site itself hasn't moved yet. That's what to watch. If it climbs over the next week or two, we'll know what press buys you on a query where free tactics have stalled.

**Where things stand**

On mold, citations are winning. On radon, three things outrank every contest site: a press release, directory profiles, and a local news listing. None of them score points, but all of them were built by contestants.

Free tactics got people indexed and onto page 2 and 3. Getting from there to page 1 is going to take something more.
