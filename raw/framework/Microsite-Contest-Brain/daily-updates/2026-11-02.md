**How these rankings are measured**

Same way every crawl since Sept 30. A regular Chrome window, English and US set, personalization off through the URL, and a location parameter that places the searcher in Gettysburg or York. Three pages for mold, four for radon, ten seconds between loads, and nothing is counted until the footer shows the town name and "Results are not personalized." Only the blue organic links count. Ads, the map pack, the AI Overview and the forum block are skipped. Each domain gets one position, its best URL, and positions 1 through 20 score. So when this post says number one, it means the first organic website on page one of Google for the exact contest query, as seen from Gettysburg, with nothing paid or personalized in the way. Tools and the built in browser preview put new sites ten to fifteen spots higher than this, which is why I don't use them.
