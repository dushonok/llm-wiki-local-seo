**Day 43: The Day After Number One**

Today's crawl, pinned to Gettysburg and York.

**Mold**

- Sunday the stacker's homepage was number one. Tuesday morning it's not in the top 30.
- The services page at 13 and the contact page at 20 are exactly where they were Sunday. Those two pages weren't touched.
- The homepage was. And so was most of the site. Monday the stacker shipped 73 new pages: 53 blog posts between 1,700 and 4,700 words, 17 new service area pages, a cost page, a process page, a state mold law page. Every township page was rewritten. The homepage went from 4,880 words to 7,103 with a new title. The site went from 47 pages to 119 overnight.
- It's the biggest single ship anyone has made in this contest, and it landed the morning after the site reached the top.
- I can't prove the rebuild is why the homepage dropped. I can say that the two pages left alone held, every page that changed moved, and this is the same site that hit 4 on Oct 12 after a 42 page republish and was at 22 the next day.
- The leader held 15 for the fifth crawl in a row. Nothing on it has changed since Oct 15.

**Radon**

- Zero for the fifth day running.
- The 26 year old company holds 5.
- The directory contestant posted a sixty second YouTube video this week and it's already at 18. The directory's own site is still nowhere.

**What Day 43 says**

- Big ships move sites. We've seen that four times. Every entry into the top 15 followed one.
- Big ships also reset the test. A page Google was ranking at number one became a different page on Monday, and Google is reading it again from the start. The 73 new pages will get read too, and if the pattern holds, something moves within 48 hours. Which direction is the question.
- The alternative was to leave number one alone for three weeks and ship the 73 pages in December. We'll never know what that would have done, which is the point.

**What to take away**

Ship big before you rank, not after. A rebuild is how you get Google's attention. Once you have it, every edit is a new thing to evaluate. The leader has held 15 for ten days by doing nothing. The stacker had number one for one day and spent the next morning doubling the site. If his homepage is back on page one by Thursday, we learned a big site can survive a rebuild. If it isn't, we learned what the leader already knew.

Twenty two days left. Mold is 14 points. Radon is zero.
