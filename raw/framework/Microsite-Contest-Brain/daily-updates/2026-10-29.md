**Day 45: The Rebuild Came Back, and the Site Nobody Entered**

Today's crawl, pinned to Gettysburg and York.

**Mold**

- Sunday the stacker's homepage was number one. Monday he shipped 73 pages and rewrote it. Tuesday it was gone. Today it's back at 15.
- That's the 48 hour pattern holding exactly. Google re read the site and placed it two days after the ship. It placed it fourteen spots lower than where it was.
- The inner pages that held 13 and 20 while the homepage was gone have dropped out. Google picked the homepage again and let the others go.
- The hyphen .com leads at 14. Sixth crawl in a row between 14 and 15. Last change on the site was Oct 15.
- A site crept up from 24 to 20 for a point. The two .nets sit at 21 and 22, the .org at 25.
- The stacker's Facebook is at 9, Clutch at 11, and a Manta listing appeared at 30. Six weeks in, his profiles still outrank his site.

**Radon**

- Zero. Seventh crawl.
- A Thumbtack page landed at 13, another trusted domain taking a spot a contest site would need.
- And there's a site at position 4 that I haven't told you about yet.

**The site nobody entered**

For a week I've been watching a radon site sit on page one that isn't on our sheet. I held off writing about it until I'd checked the records. Here's what they say.

- The business behind it is real. A York radon company that's been operating since 1999, on the state's certified mitigator list, BBB member, with a Business Profile carrying over 200 reviews that go back years.
- The website is brand new. The domain was registered Sept 17, two days before our deadline. The first copy of the site appeared Sept 18. The company's old domain, from 2013, is now parked for sale.
- It's built the way contest sites are built. Same kind of schema, same style of profile stack, same week as everyone else. And the press releases about the company were issued by a directory that belongs to one of our contestants. I'll leave it there.
- The day Google connected that fresh domain to the old company, it went to page one. It has held 4 to 7 for nine days. It's also the first source Google's AI Overview cites for the radon search. No contestant's radon site has held any position for nine days, and none of them appear in the overview at all.

It's not on the sheet, so it can't score, and the two domain rule means it couldn't anyway. But it's the most instructive site in this contest, because it's the control group we never planned for: a contest style build attached to a real business instead of a new one. Same pages, same profiles, same timing. One difference. It held, and the AI picked it.

**What Day 45 says**

- A rebuild from number one is a reset. The stacker got his 48 hour re read and came back at 15. The 73 new pages may pay off in a month. The position they cost is the position the contest is scored on.
- The leader has held by doing nothing for two weeks.
- The strongest radon site in the market is a six week old domain with a 26 year old business behind it, and it's winning the blue links and the AI answer. Everything else is a six week old domain with a six week old business behind it. That gap is the whole contest, and it's the same gap in the AI Overview that it is in the rankings.

**What to take away**

Two lessons, one for each query. On mold: if you're at the top, the rebuild can wait until November 19. If you're in the block, ship it now, because the re read is the only lever that has moved a site here. On radon: the site that's winning borrowed a real business. You can't do that inside the rules, but you can do the next best thing, which is make your site describe a business Google can confirm in ten places. The AI Overview is reading the same signals the rankings are. A business Google can confirm gets cited. A domain it can't gets skipped.

Twenty days left. Mold is 14 points. Radon is zero.
