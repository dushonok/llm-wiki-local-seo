The citation site is back on top at 10. It now owns 10, 11 and 12 outright: the site, its MapQuest listing, its Clutch profile. Plus its Facebook page at 6. Four of the top 12 results belong to one contestant.

The site that took the lead two days ago fell to 23. Here's the thing to notice. Its ranking URL has changed every single day this week. FAQ page, then a township page, then the FAQ page again. **Radon**

Zero points again. Seven of the last eight days.

Page 1 now has two news-style results from this contest. The press release at 5, and a second article dated September 30 at 9. A third copy of the release sits at 13. The directory site that paid for all of it is out of the top 50.

The citation stacker's profiles are at 8, 17, 22 and 27. Their site is at 24.

**What this week taught me**

Three things keep repeating.

One. A site that holds a position with one consistent page is more stable than a site whose ranking page keeps changing. If Google is rotating between your pages, you have two or three pages competing for the same query. Pick one, point the others at it.

Two. Off site assets rank before the site does. Profiles, press, listings. On radon they're the only contest results on page 1. On mold the leader has three of them around its own listing.

Three. Nobody has cracked radon. The best any contest site has done under the correct method is 17, for one day. If you're on that query, you're not behind. Everyone is.
