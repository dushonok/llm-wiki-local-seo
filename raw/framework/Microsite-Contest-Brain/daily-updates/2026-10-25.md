**Day 41: The Site That Came Back**

Today's crawl, pinned to Gettysburg and York.

**Mold**

- A contest site is on page one. Position 9, fourteen points. First time a mold site has touched page one since Oct 12, when one lasted a day.
- It's the .net. If you've been here since September you remember it. It led the board for the first two weeks, back when we measured without the location pin. Then it fell out of the top 50 and stayed gone for ten days. It crept back in at 44 on Day 31. Today it's 9.
- Here's what makes it different from every other jump we've seen. Nothing shipped. The sitemap was last touched Oct 2. Every previous entry into the top 15 came within 48 hours of a big republish. This one climbed 35 spots over ten days on a site that didn't change.
- What it has: the longest home page in the contest at 5,474 words. 95 pages. A street address, a local phone, and seven profiles in its schema. Links out to the EPA, the CDC, Energy Star and the industry's duct cleaning association. It is, page for page, the most complete build in the field, and it's been that way since September.
- So the story isn't a rebuild. The story is that Google tested it in week two, took it off the board for ten days, and brought it back higher than before. That's the "let it cook" rule showing up in the data for the first time.
- Below it, the leader held 15. The stacker slid to 19. Everything else is where it was.

**Radon**

- Zero for the fourth crawl running. No contest site in the top 40.
- The 26 year old company's new domain moved up to 5.

**Two things to take from Day 41**

- Every site that jumped before this one did it with a ship and gave it back within a week. The .net did it without a ship, after being gone ten days, with the most complete entity in the contest behind it. Watch whether it holds. If it does, it's the first site to earn page one rather than visit it.
- Being gone from the top 50 is not the end. The .net was out for ten days and the people who built it didn't tear it down. They left it alone. That might be the most useful thing anyone in this contest has done.

Twenty four days left. Mold is 22 points, radon is zero, and the board has a new leader.
