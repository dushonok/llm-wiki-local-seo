**Day 18: New Leader, and What They Changed**

Ran today's crawl pinned to Gettysburg and York. The top of the board flipped overnight.

**Mold**

A site that was at 22 yesterday is at 10 today. That's a 12 spot jump in one day, and it passed the citation heavy site that had been leading. Those two are now at 10 and 11. Nobody else is in the top 20.

**Radon**

The same contestant's radon site went from 32 to 17. First real radon points of the contest. They are now the only person scoring on both queries.

**What they did**

Both sites were republished on October 1. I compared them against my crawl from two weeks ago. Five changes:

1. **Cut the filler pages.** Privacy policy, terms, disclaimer, accessibility statement and a couple of other legal pages came out of the sitemap. Google had been ranking those pages instead of the real ones. Fewer pages, better result.
2. **Added a cost page** with pricing marked up in schema.
3. **Added FAQ and Service schema.** Neither site had them before. The FAQ page is now the one ranking at 10.
4. **Rewrote the titles.** Keyword first, "Free Estimates" at the end.
5. **Fresh modified date on every page.**

They also built a handful of directory listings last week. Manta, BBB, MerchantCircle and a few others.

None of that cost money. It was a cleanup and a republish.

**Other movement**

- The 107 page township grid site fell 18 spots and out of the points. Google swapped its homepage for its contact page.
- Two sites that were in the 20s and 40s yesterday are gone from the top 50.
- The press release from earlier this week now ranks twice on radon, at 5 and 13, on two different news sites. The directory site behind it still hasn't moved.

**Takeaway**

More pages is not the goal. The right pages is. This contestant got smaller and moved up. If Google keeps picking your contact page, your privacy policy or your thank you page over your homepage, you have pages that shouldn't be indexed. Pull them from the sitemap, noindex them, and republish.

**Scoreboard**

- Mold: 2 sites scoring, 22 points, best position 10
- Radon: 1 site scoring, 4 points, best position 17
