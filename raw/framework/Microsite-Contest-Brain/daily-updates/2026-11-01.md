**How these rankings are measured**

Same process every crawl since Sept 30. A normal Chrome window, English and US set, personalization switched off in the URL, and a location parameter that tells Google the searcher is in Gettysburg or York. Pages one through three on mold, one through four on radon, ten seconds between loads, and I check the footer for the town name and "Results are not personalized" before counting anything. Blue links only. Ads, the map pack, the AI Overview and the forum block are skipped. A domain gets the position of its best URL. A member made the fair point this week that this isn't the same as standing in the town. It isn't. It's Google's own simulation of a searcher there, run the same way every day, which is why the movement is real even if a phone on Baltimore Street might show a spot or two of difference.

**What to take away**

- A rebuild from number one cost five crawls, not the contest. Six days later the site is back on top with more of it ranking. If you're sitting on a finished site and afraid to touch it, this is the evidence that shipping a big batch at once is survivable. Trickling pages still hasn't moved anyone.
- Three URLs from one domain in the top 20 is new. 119 pages gave Google more doors to walk through. The 18 page site holds 15 with one URL and nothing else. Both score. Only one is growing.
- Radon has 17 days and nobody in it. The profiles rank, the shorts rank, the sites don't. Same advice as Friday: build the ten places that agree and let Google connect them.
