**How these rankings are measured**

Same process since Sept 30. A normal Chrome window, English and US set, personalization turned off in the URL, and a location code that places the search in Gettysburg or York. Three result pages for mold, four for radon, ten seconds between loads, seven loads a day. Nothing counts until the footer shows the town name and "Results are not personalized." Only the regular blue links count, so ads, the map box, the AI Overview and the forum block are skipped. One position per domain, its best URL. Positions 1 through 20 score, and only domains on the contest sheet can score. "Number one" means the first organic website on page one for the exact contest query from the contest town.

**Glossary for newcomers**

- **Crawl:** my daily check of where every site ranks. Pinned means I search as if I were standing in Gettysburg or York, with my own search history turned off, so everyone's measured the same way.
- **Position:** where a site appears in the regular blue links. Position 1 is the first website on page one. Positions 11 to 20 are page two. Ads, the map box and the AI answer at the top don't count.
- **Points:** only positions 1 to 20 score. First is 50 points, 20th is 1 point, 21st and below is zero. Each query is worth up to 50, and only a site's best page counts.
- **Eligible:** on the contest sheet, a fresh domain registered after Sept 15 and submitted before Sept 20. Sites that rank but aren't eligible can't score, but they can occupy scoring positions.
- **Profiles or listings:** a business's pages on other sites, like Facebook, BBB, Patch, Houzz or MapQuest. They often show up on Google before the website does.
- **Entity:** Google's sense that a real business exists behind a website, built from the profiles, phone number, address and reviews all agreeing with each other.
- **The stacker:** the contestant whose mold site is number one, nicknamed for building about 15 business profiles in the first week after launch.
- **The hyphen .com:** the mold site in second place, named for the dashes in its domain.
- **Keystone, the .org, the pa .com, the matrix pair, the Patch pair, the late pair, the help pair, the directory contestant, the 116 page site, the single entries:** nicknames for other contestants' sites so nobody's domain is revealed before the contest ends.
- **The never entered site:** a York radon company's new website that is not in the contest and can't win, but ranks number one for radon.
- **Ship or batch:** publishing many new pages at once. **Let it cook:** stop editing and wait a few weeks for Google to decide.
