**Day 10 Check: A Directory Just Debuted in the Points**

Crawled both queries again this morning. Three things worth your attention.

**1. A brand new site landed at position 16 on its first day**

Highest debut of the contest so far. It's not a fake contractor site. It's a directory. Eleven profiles of the real local companies, links out to every one of them, a handful of service guides, and links to the town and county government sites.

Every other contest site is trying to beat Servpro and Paul Davis. This one lists them. Google apparently likes that a lot more on day one.

That debut pushed five contest sites down a spot. Nobody did anything wrong, a new competitor just cut in line.

**2. Downtime costs rankings fast, and they come back fast**

One mold site's server went down on Tuesday. Google dropped its homepage the same day and it fell from 18 to 33. Server came back Wednesday. Today it's at 20.

Check your uptime. One bad day is a page of rankings.

**3. Radon is still zero points, and the citations are now the problem**

Every contest site on radon slid another 2 to 9 spots. Aggregators own pages 1 and 2.

Two contestants built profile stacks and it worked. Their Houzz, Pinterest, Manta and Yellow Pages listings are ranking ahead of their own sites. That's the good news.

The bad news is both of them have address mismatches now. One listing shows a different street than the site schema. One snippet shows a different address than the homepage. Google is displaying the conflict right in the results.

Profiles work. Inconsistent profiles work against you. Pick one address, one phone, one name, and use it everywhere or don't build them.

Two and a half weeks until the first month closes. Mold is a footrace right now. Radon is going to need backlinks.
