**How these rankings are measured**

The same way since Sept 30. I search in a normal Chrome window with the location set to Gettysburg or York and personalization turned off. I check three pages of results for mold and four for radon, ten seconds apart. I don't count anything until the bottom of the page shows the town name and says the results are not personalized.

Only the regular website links count. Ads, the map box, the AI answer and the forum box are skipped. Each site gets one position, its best page. Positions 1 to 20 score, and only sites on the contest sheet can score.

**What to take away**

**Glossary for newcomers**

**Crawl:** my daily check of where every site ranks, done as if I were standing in the town.

**Position:** where a site appears in the regular results. Position 1 is the first website on page one. Positions 11 to 20 are page two.

**Points:** first place is 50 points and 20th place is 1 point. Below 20th is zero.

**Listing or profile:** a business's page on another site, like Facebook, MapQuest, Patch or the BBB.

**Press release:** a short announcement sent to news sites. It appears on their domains, which Google already trusts.

**The stacker:** the contestant whose mold site is number one. They built about 15 listings in the first week.

**The hyphen .com:** the mold site in second place, named for the dashes in its domain.

**The Patch pair:** a contestant whose Patch listings ranked long before their sites did.

**The directory contestant:** a contestant whose radon site is a directory of real contractors.

**The radon guide and the help pair:** two contest radon sites that have come closest to scoring this week.

**The never entered site:** a real York radon company's new website. It isn't in the contest and can't win.
