**Day 40: Visited, Not Ranked**

Today's crawl, pinned to Gettysburg and York.

**Radon**

- The site that held position 7 on Monday and Wednesday is out of the top 40 today.
- Nothing changed on it. Pages are the same, site is up. Google tested it for four days and put it back.
- That makes three sites that have reached page one in this contest and three that left within a week.
- Position 7 now belongs to a brand new domain for a York radon company that's been in business since 1999, with a Business Profile holding 225 reviews. The domain is five weeks old. The business is 26 years old.
- The day Google connected that domain to that business, it went to page one. The contest site went to page five.
- Zero radon points. Stacker's site at 36.

**Mold**

- Eligible points fell from 19 to 11. The .org dropped from 16 to 27. The 116 page site fell out of the top 30 again.
- The leader held 15. The stacker moved up to 17 and bought another press release, now at 12. Keystone is back at 20 for a point.
- Nine of the top 20 results are contestant profiles. Four are on page one: a Facebook page, a Patch listing, a BBB profile and a second Facebook page.
- Page one of mold has more contestant profiles on it than page two has contestant websites.

**What 40 days say**

- Four sites have reached the top 15. None held it more than seven days.
- Every profile built in week three is still where it was. Every press release is still on page one.
- The sites rise and fall. The off site assets don't move.
- On radon, the moment a real business with history showed up on a readable domain, it took the spot. On mold, Google doesn't seem to know anyone, which is why a six week old site with no phone number still leads.

**What to do with that**

- Don't celebrate a pop. Write it down and check again in a week.
- Profiles and press hold. Build them. Patch, Facebook, BBB, Houzz, Clutch and a regional press release are all on pages one and two right now.
- Then make the site look like the business the profiles describe: name, address, phone, ten places that agree. Almost nobody in this contest has done it, and it's the only thing that turned a five week old domain into a page one result today.

Twenty five days left. Radon is zero. Mold is six points.
