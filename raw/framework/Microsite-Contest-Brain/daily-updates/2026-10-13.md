**Day 29: Four Contestants Have Points. Here's How Each Got There.**

Today's crawl, pinned to Gettysburg and York. Yesterday one site was on page 1 at position 4. Today it's at 21. More on that at the end. First, the four people scoring right now.

**1. The citation stacker. 7 points. Radon site at 14.**

The only contestant scoring on radon, and the only one who's scored on both queries. Their playbook from day one: same static site template on both domains, one business identity, 27 profiles across Facebook, Houzz, Clutch, Manta, Crunchbase, Pinterest, YouTube and more, all declared in the site's schema. Built in week one, then left alone.

The radon site's path: indexed in the 30s, vanished in week two while its Houzz profile sat on page 1, crept back to 24 in week three, hovered at 21 and 22 for four days, then moved to 13 and has held 13 to 15 since. Google now ranks three pages from that domain.

**2. The .org pair. 3 points. Mold site at 18.**

Day one registrant. Lead gen framing, township pages, a full legal page set. Built citations in week two: Manta, BBB, MerchantCircle, ProvenExpert and a few small directories, none declared in schema.

Their big move was October 1. They pulled the privacy, terms, disclaimer and accessibility pages out of the sitemap, added a cost page, added FAQ and Service schema, rewrote titles, republished everything. The mold site jumped to 10 the next day and held two days. Since then it's bounced between 17 and 33 because Google keeps changing which page it ranks. Today it's the FAQ page at 18. Their BBB profile is at 15, three spots above it.

**3. The Keystone pair. 2 points. Mold site at 19.**

A referral site with a team page. Indexed late, debuted in the teens, then spent two weeks bouncing between 20 and 38. The ranking URL alternates between the homepage and the meet the team page. No profiles that I can find. This is the one contestant in the points on structure alone, and they've been trending up for a week: 38, 21, 23, 19.

**4. The question hub pair. 1 point. Mold site at 20.**

The opposite build from the township grid sites. Zero location pages. Sixty question pages instead: does bleach kill mold, does insurance cover it, mold in closets, mold under carpet. Every page links from a hub on the homepage. Eight profiles declared, including Manta, Crunchbase and ProvenExpert. The site sat in the 40s for two weeks, then went 23, 20 over the last two days. Slowest climb of any scorer, and the first time a pure content site has scored.

**The one that fell**

The stacker's mold site went 22, 4, 21 over three days. Nothing changed on the site. Google tried it on page 1 for a day and put it back. The gap between "tried at 4" and "stays at 4" is almost certainly links, because everything else that site could do is already done.

**What the four have in common**

- All four have the business name matching the domain and the site
- All four have a street address and phone in schema
- Three of four have profiles, and the one without is the most volatile
- None of them touched their site this week

And what separates them from the 29 contestants with zero: mostly the profiles, and a month of leaving it alone.
