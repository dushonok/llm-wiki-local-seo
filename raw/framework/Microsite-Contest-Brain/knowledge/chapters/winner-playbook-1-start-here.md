**The Winner's Playbook: Start Here**

**TLDR:** The winner of the contest wrote down everything he did and gave us his working sheet. This folder turns it into checklists, trackers and worksheets you can print and use on your own site.

**What this is**

Zeeshan Y. won the contest with gettysburgmoldremediationpros.com. Everything in the Results folder describes his site from the outside, because that was all we could see.

After the contest he sent us the inside view. A full write up of his launch process, day by day. And the sheet he used to log every listing, every social post and every link.

He told us we could share all of it. So we did.

**Who built it**

Zeeshan has been building rank and rent sites for two years. This was not his first, and it shows in how fast he moved and in the order he did things.

Keep that in mind when you read that the site was live in four hours. If this is your first site, follow the same steps in the same order and give it a weekend.

**Three things this changes**

**He did not stop working. He stopped touching the site.** We told you the last weeks at number one came from leaving the site alone. That is true of the site. For the last 25 days all of his work was off the site: more listings, more links and weekly social posts.

**The profile count was far higher than we could see.** His site code lists 16 profiles, and that was the number we reported. His sheet logs 63, and his write up says 70. He built 20 to 25 in the first days and added 5 to 10 every week.

**The big blog batch was an accident.** On October 27 the site went from 47 pages to 119. We treated that as a planned update. He calls it "a big mistake." The posts were meant to be scheduled and they all went live together.

**What the money looks like**

He sells the calls through Lead Smart. So far he has had over 30 calls, many of them spam or robocalls, and leads worth $214 have converted.

That is a number one ranking in a small town, a few weeks in. It is a real result and a modest one.
