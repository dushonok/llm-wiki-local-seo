**The Number One Site's Profile Checklist**

Lesson one from the contest was that listings rank before websites do. This page turns that into a build list.

The winning site, built by Zeeshan Y., lists 16 profiles in its own code. After the contest he shared his own sheet, which logs 63. The full list is in The Winner's Playbook folder, under The Citation List. On 26 checks we recorded every profile and listing that showed up for the two contest searches. The attached PDF is a tick list you can work through for your own site.

**What the winner's profiles did**

Of his 16 profiles, three showed up in Google's top 30 on their own: MapQuest, Facebook and Manta. Two of those reached page one.

The other 13 never appeared for the search. That includes his Yelp, Houzz, YouTube, Pinterest, X, TikTok, Reddit and Quora profiles.

So most profiles do not rank by themselves. They still give Google more places that agree on who the business is.

**What ranked for other contestants**

Some of the strongest listings in the contest belonged to other sites.

A Facebook page and a Patch listing were each on page one on all 25 checks we saw them. A free BBB profile was on page one on most checks. A Houzz profile was the best listing on the radon search. One short YouTube video, titled with the search, showed up on almost every check.

Press releases reached page one within days, and usually faded within days. Treat them as a visit, not a listing.

**The order to build them in**

Start with the ten kinds of profile that Google showed during the contest: Facebook, Houzz, Patch, MapQuest, BBB, YouTube, Birdeye, Pinterest, Manta, and a press release if you want one.

Then build the rest of the winner's set: Yelp, Clutch, ProvenExpert, Hotfrog, Cybo, TripAdvisor, X, TikTok, Reddit and Quora.

The PDF lists each one with a tick box, what to create, and how it performed.

**The rules that matter more than the list**

Use the same business name, spelled the same way, on every profile. Use the same phone number and the same town. Link every one back to your home page, with the same short description and logo.

Save every profile link in one sheet as you go, then add those links to your site's code. The winner also shows five of them in his footer.

Build them in your first week, then leave them alone.

**Do they need upkeep?**

No. The listings that ranked in the contest were fill in once profiles. They can be thin, as long as every field is complete and matches the site. One post on a social page, or one short video on YouTube, is enough to start.

**What this does and does not prove**

Only three of the winner's 16 profiles ranked on their own, so we cannot say which ones helped his site. What we can say is that the one site with this full set finished at number one, and the second place site, which listed no profiles at all, never reached page one.
