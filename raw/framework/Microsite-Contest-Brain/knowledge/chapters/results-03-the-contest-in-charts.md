**The Contest in Charts**

The Final Results page shows where everyone finished. This page shows how they got there. The attached PDF has one chart for each search, with every scoring site drawn as a line from the first check to the last.

**How to read the charts**

Each line is one website. The top of the chart is position 1 on Google, so a line going up is a site climbing.

The green band is positions 1 to 20. Any site inside it on a given day was scoring points.

The grey strip on the left covers our first week of checks, before we started setting the search location to the town. Treat those early points as rough.

Where a line stops and starts again, we had no check for more than four days.

**What to look for on the mold chart**

Eleven entered sites scored at least once in the final month. For most of the contest they sat together in a pack on page two.

On October 26 one line leaves the pack and goes straight to number one. That is Zeeshan Y.'s site. It drops back for five days after a big update, returns to the top on November 1, and stays there as a flat line to the end.

Just under it, the green line is Cyril G.'s site. It never reached page one, but look at how steady it is. It sat between 13 and 17 for a month while everything below it bounced around.

The lines at the bottom of the green band are the story for everyone else. They drift in and out of the points from one check to the next.

**What to look for on the radon chart**

The dashed line is the site that was never entered, built by Blair W. It first appears at 7 in late October and climbs to number one in early November.

One entered site jumps onto page one for two checks in October and then falls away. That is the only time an entered radon site scored in the final month.

Every other entered radon site spends November between 23 and 40. The lines move around each other, but none of them gets into the green band.

**If every check had counted**

The prizes were decided by the final check alone. The last page of the PDF asks a different question: what if we had added up the points from all 26 full checks?

Zeeshan Y. would have 937 points. Cyril G. would have 166. Nick C. would be third with 43, from two different sites. Kate S., who took third place on the final check, would be fourth with 16.

That table is there to show who was consistent. A site that scores on 26 checks out of 26 has a ranking. A site that scores on two has had two good days.
