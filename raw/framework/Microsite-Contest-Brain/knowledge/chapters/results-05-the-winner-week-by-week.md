**The Winner, Week by Week**

This page follows one site from the day its domain was registered to the final check. The site is gettysburgmoldremediationpros.com, built by Zeeshan Y., and it finished the contest at number one on Google.

Read it for the shape, not the dates. Almost every site that ends up ranking goes through a slide like the one in the middle of this story. Knowing that in advance is what stops you from breaking a site that is about to work.

**Week 1: launch and profiles**

The domain was registered on September 15. The site was live four hours later, and 20 to 25 profiles and listings were built for it in the first days.

**Weeks 2 and 3: a quick climb**

On our early checks the site sat between 13 and 18. By the start of October it had climbed to 10, which is the bottom of page one.

This is the part that fools people. A new site showing up on page one in week three feels like it has arrived. It had not.

**Weeks 4 and 5: the slide**

The site slid back to 17, 18 and 19 and stayed there for about two weeks.

Nothing was wrong with it. Google was still deciding. The builder made two small updates in this stretch and otherwise waited.

This is where most people start rewriting pages, changing titles and adding content in a panic. If you take one thing from this page, take this: the slide is normal.

**Week 6: number one**

After four more checks at 17 to 19, the site went straight to number one on October 26. That was 41 days after the domain was registered. The site had 47 pages at the time.

**Week 7: the big update and the dip**

The next day the site grew from 47 pages to 119 in a single update. It dropped from 1 to 13. He has since told us this was an accident. The posts were meant to be scheduled.

It sat at 13, 15 and 12 for the next few checks. Five days after the update it was back at number one.

A big update costs you a few days. It did not cost this site its ranking, and it came back with more of its pages showing up in Google than before.

**Weeks 8 and 9: no edits**

From November 1 to the final check on November 17, the site was number one on every check. That is 17 in a row.

The builder did not edit the site once in that time. He has since told us all of his work in that stretch was off the site: listings, links and social posts.

**What to take from it**

The first ranking is not the real one. The slide after it is not a failure. A big update will dip before it recovers. And the longest stretch at the top came from leaving the site alone.

**How we know**

Positions from September 30 on come from searches with the location set to Gettysburg and personalization turned off. The few checks before that were not location pinned, and the chart marks them in grey. We have no checks between October 5 and October 15, so the line on the chart is broken there. Update dates come from the site's own sitemap.
