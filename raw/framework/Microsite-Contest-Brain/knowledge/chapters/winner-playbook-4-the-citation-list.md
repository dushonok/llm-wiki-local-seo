**The Citation List**

**TLDR:** We told you the winner had 16 profiles. He had 63. Part 4 of the playbook PDF lists every one as a tick list, grouped so you know where to start.

**Why our number was wrong**

His site code names 16 profiles as its own. That was all we could see from the outside, and it is what the Profile Checklist in the Results folder is built on.

His working sheet logs 63 profiles and listings. His write up puts the total at 70.

**How he built them**

He built 20 to 25 main local listings in the first days, before any other off-page work.

Each one was sent to an indexing tool the day it was made. He used Rapid Indexer. We have not tested it.

After that he added 5 to 10 new ones every week, for the whole contest. He never stopped.

**The five groups**

**Social profiles.** Six of them: Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, TikTok and Pinterest.

**Well known listing sites.** Fifteen, including Yelp, MapQuest, Houzz, Tripadvisor, Manta and Hotfrog. Most of the 16 in his code are in this group.

**Profile and publishing sites.** Seven, including Medium, Tumblr, Issuu and Linktree.

**General business directories.** Twenty smaller free directories. This is where the weekly 5 to 10 came from.

**One family of sister directories.** Fifteen sites that share one layout and one address pattern. Our reading is that they are one network, so count them as one signal and not fifteen.

**What we found when we opened them**

On November 25 our script could read 35 of the 63 pages. Every one of those linked to his site. On 23 the link was a normal one and on 12 it was marked nofollow. The rest block scripts or build their pages in the browser, so we could not read them.

Only three of the 63 ever showed up on Google for the contest search: MapQuest, Facebook and Manta. MapQuest reached 6 and Facebook reached 9.

So we cannot tell you which listings mattered. We can tell you he treated them as a set and kept adding to it.

**A few are a poor fit**

One is a directory for the United Arab Emirates. One listing sits under a car fleet category. Do not copy those. Pick the right category, and skip a directory that has nothing to do with your country or trade.

**The one rule**

Use exactly the same business name, phone number and address on every listing as on your site.
