**Final Results**

This page covers how the contest finished: who placed, where every ranked site ended up, and what happened on each of the two searches. The full results are in the PDF attached to this page.

**How the winners were decided**

The winners were decided by one thing: where each entered site ranked on Google on the final check, November 17.

First place on Google was worth 50 points, second 40, third 34, and so on down to 1 point for 20th. A contestant's score was the total across the sites they entered. Only sites on the official entry sheet could score.

**The winners**

**1st place, $1,000: Zeeshan Y.** His site, gettysburgmoldremediationpros.com, finished at number one on Google for "mold remediation gettysburg pa." That earned 50 points. It had held number one for 17 checks in a row.

**2nd place, $500: Cyril G.** His site, mold-remediation-gettysburg-pa.com, finished at position 17 for 4 points. It scored on every one of the last 26 checks.

**3rd place, $250: Kate S.** Her site, gettysburgpamoldremediation.com, finished at position 19 for 2 points. It climbed back into the points on the very last check.

**Honorable mention: Blair W.** He built absoluteradonmitigationllc.com, which finished at number one on Google for "radon mitigation york pa." It is the website of a real York radon company and was never entered, so it could not score. In the daily updates it was the "mystery site."

**What happened on mold**

Eight entered mold sites were in the top 30 on the final check. Three of them were in the top 20 and scored. The next five finished between 21 and 28, just outside the points.

The site at 21 had scored on three of the last six checks. That is how close the bottom of the scoring zone was all the way to the end.

**What happened on radon**

No entered radon site finished in the top 20, so no radon points were awarded. All three prizes were won on mold.

Five entered radon sites were in the top 40 on the final check. The closest finished at 30, 31 and 32.

Why radon was so much harder than mold is covered in Five Lessons.

**How the rankings were measured**

Every check used the same method. We searched in a normal Chrome window with the location set to Gettysburg or York and personalization turned off. Ads, the map box and the AI answer were not counted, and each site was counted once at its best page.
