**The 48 Hour Launch**

**TLDR:** He bought the domain on September 15 and had a full site live four hours later. Town pages went up on day two. Part 2 of the playbook PDF is his exact order of steps as a tick list.

**Research came first, and it took two hours**

Before anything was built, he searched the main keyword and wrote down who was ranking. He pulled every secondary keyword out of the competitor pages.

From that he made two lists. One of services, which became his service pages. One of neighborhoods and nearby towns, which became his area pages.

He ended up with 9 service pages. He has 36 area pages today.

**Then the build**

He made a GitHub repository, connected Claude Code to VS Code, and gave it the main keyword. Claude Code built the home page, every service page and every trust page.

His trust pages are About, Contact, FAQ, Careers, Warranty, Privacy, Terms and Accessibility. He also has a cost guide, a process page and a page on Pennsylvania mold laws.

**Then the content**

This is the part most people skip. He did not publish what Claude wrote first time. For each page he studied the pages already ranking, wrote an outline, and only then had Claude write the final copy. The next page in this folder covers that method.

**Then live**

He connected the domain to Cloudflare and published on Vercel. Four hours from the first step.

**Day two was the town pages**

He built the neighborhood pages from his day one list and had Claude write content for each place. He calls this vibe coding, which just means telling Claude what he wanted, page by page.

**Days one to three, off the site**

The social profiles were created. The first 20 to 25 listings were built and sent to an indexing tool. The first social posts went out on day three.

**Days four to seven, the first check**

He waited three to four days and then looked at what Google had indexed, page by page. He found the wrong page ranking for his main keyword and fixed it. He redirected one or two thin town pages to the home page.

**Read this before you try to match the clock**

He did this in four hours, with two years of rank and rent sites behind him. If this is your first, give it a weekend. The order is what matters: research, build, content, live, towns, listings.
