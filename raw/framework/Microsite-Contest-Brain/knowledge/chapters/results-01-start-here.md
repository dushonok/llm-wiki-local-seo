**The Microsite $1,000 SEO Contest: Start Here**

This module holds everything from the Rank Expand Academy microsite contest: who won, what we learned, and the checklists and guides that came out of it. This first page is the overview. Each page after it goes deeper into one piece.

**What the contest was**

Members built brand new websites and competed to rank them on Google for two local searches. One was "mold remediation gettysburg pa" and the other was "radon mitigation york pa."

It ran from September 15 to November 18, 2026. Thirty two members entered 61 websites.

**The rules in short**

Every site had to be on a new domain registered on or after September 15 and entered by September 20. Each contestant could enter up to two sites.

Sites earned points for where they ranked in the regular Google results. First place was worth 50 points, second 40, third 34, and so on down to 1 point for 20th. Anything below 20th earned nothing.

We checked the rankings with the search location set to each town, so we saw what a local customer would see. The winners were decided by the final ranking check on November 17.

The full rules are here: https://www.skool.com/rank-expand-academy/microsite-1000-seo-contest-official-rules

**Who won**

**1st place, $1,000: Zeeshan Y.** His mold site finished at number one on Google and held that spot for the last 17 checks in a row.

**2nd place, $500: Cyril G.** His mold site finished at position 17.

**3rd place, $250: Kate S.** Her mold site finished at position 19.

**Honorable mention: Blair W.** He built the site that finished at number one on Google for the radon search. It is the website of a real York radon company and was never entered in the contest, so it could not score. It still beat every other result on the page, and it was the "mystery site" in the daily updates.

All three prizes were won on the mold search. No entered site finished in the top 20 for radon, and the reasons for that turned out to be one of the biggest lessons of the contest.
