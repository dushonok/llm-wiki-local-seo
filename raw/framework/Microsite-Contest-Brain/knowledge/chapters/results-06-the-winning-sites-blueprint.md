**The Winning Site's Blueprint**

The last page showed when the winning site ranked. This page shows what it is made of.

The site is gettysburgmoldremediationpros.com, built by Zeeshan Y. It finished the contest at number one and was last changed on October 27. Use this as a reference for structure. The words on your own site have to be your own.

**The size of it**

The site has 119 pages and about 220,000 words of content.

That breaks down into one home page, nine service pages, 36 town pages, 58 articles and four guides. The rest are overview pages that list the services, towns and articles, plus the usual about, contact and legal pages.

**The home page did the ranking**

The page that held number one was the home page. It runs to about 6,800 words under 18 main headings, answers nine questions, and includes three tables.

It also links out to eight outside sources, including the borough and county websites, the EPA and FEMA flood maps. The quote form at the top asks for six things: name, phone, email, town, service and a message.

**One page for each job, one page for each town**

Each of the nine service pages covers a different job, such as attic mold, basement mold or crawl space mold, at about 2,000 words each.

Each of the 36 town pages runs to about 1,700 words and was written separately. When we compared them, the town pages shared about 29 percent of their text. On the member sites we audit, town pages built from a template usually share 50 to 70 percent.

The 58 articles each answer one question and share only about 5 percent of their text with each other.

**How the pages connect**

The home page links to every service and every town, and to dozens of articles. The important pages are all one click from the home page.

Every service page links to the other eight and back to the home page. Every article links to about eight other articles and a couple of service pages. The menu lists every service by name on every page.

**What is in the code**

The home page describes the business to Google in one block: its name, phone, address, opening hours, the towns it serves, its list of services, and links to 16 profiles. Most pages also mark up their questions and answers as FAQs.

**How it was launched**

The site reached number one with 47 pages. The next day it grew to 119 in a single update: 53 articles, 17 town pages and 3 guides. It dipped to 13, returned to number one five days later, and was not edited again.

**Where it goes further than the Microsite Blueprint**

The winner built more than our Blueprint asks for: a much longer home page, nine service pages, 36 separately written town pages, a 58 article blog, and a street address shown on the site.

Keep that in proportion. One site is one result. The second place site had 18 pages and held page two for a month, so size alone does not explain the win.

**What is in the PDF**

**How we know**

The figures come from a full copy of the site saved on October 27. The live site still matched it at the end of the contest. Word counts cover the main content of each page and leave out menus and footers.
