**The Social Posting Log**

**TLDR:** He logged 82 social posts across four accounts in eight weeks. Never more than four a week on any one account, and never on a weekend. Part 6 of the playbook PDF has the pattern, every post title, and a four week planner.

**The numbers**

He logged 25 posts on X, 24 on TikTok, 24 on Pinterest and 9 on Facebook. He posted on 29 different days between September 17 and November 10.

The first posts went out on day three, before the site had ranked for anything.

**First, one post per service page**

For the first two and a half weeks, each post carried the name of one of his service pages. Basement mold. Mold inspection and testing. Black mold removal. Attic. Crawl space. Water damage. Air ducts. Commercial.

The same title went out on X, TikTok and Pinterest on the same day. One piece of work, three places.

**Then, one problem per post**

From October 8 the posts change. Each one opens with something a homeowner notices. A musty smell. A stain on the ceiling. Mold under the sink.

The wording is now different on each platform. These are the same kind of problem based topics his blog uses.

**Facebook was slower**

Only nine posts, starting in week two. The later ones are before and after posts.

It was also the only one of his four accounts that showed up on Google for the contest search. It reached position 9.

**Is it hard to keep up?**

A member asked this during the contest, and now we have a real answer. At his peak it was about 13 posts a week across all four accounts, and most of those were the same post in three places. By the end it was two.

That is a small weekly task, not a job.

**What we cannot tell you**

We cannot measure what the posts did for his ranking. What the log shows is a habit: a small, steady amount, kept up for eight weeks.
