## Reported, not recommended

Guest posts placed on other sites, a distributed press release, pages built to hold a link and an indexing tool are what he did. He does not say what was paid for. Our reading is that the guest posts were paid placements. Google's spam policies treat paid links and pages built only to hold a link as link schemes. Two of four guest posts were gone within a week of the contest. This is the least durable part of his plan.
