# Corrections log

Things that were said during the contest, in daily updates or early handouts, that turned out to be wrong or incomplete. If an older file in this pack disagrees with this log or with `BRAIN.md`, the older file is wrong.

## Corrected by the winner's own account (received November 25)

| What we said | What is true |
|---|---|
| The winner built about 15 profiles in his first week. | He built 20 to 25 listings in the first days and added 5 to 10 a week. His sheet logs 63. Only 16 are named in his site code, which is what we could see. |
| The last weeks at number one came from doing nothing. | The site was not edited. He worked off the site the whole time: listings, links and social posts. |
| The jump from 47 to 119 pages on October 27 was a planned update. | It was an accident. More than 50 blog posts were meant to be scheduled and went live at once. |
| If you add a large set of pages, publish it in one go. | Schedule it. The one batch in the contest was a mistake by the builder's own account. |
