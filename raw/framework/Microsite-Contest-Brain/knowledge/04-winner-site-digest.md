## Trust claims on the site

The header and footer say: fully insured, a one year written warranty, written on site estimates, and work to the IICRC S520 standard. The site shows no reviews, ratings or testimonials.

Our reading: a lead generation site has to be able to back claims like insurance and a warranty through the business that takes the calls.
