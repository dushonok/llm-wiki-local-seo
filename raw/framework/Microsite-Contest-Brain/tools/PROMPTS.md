# Prompts to use with this pack

Paste `LLM-INSTRUCTIONS.md` first and load `BRAIN.md`. Then:

**1. Judge a market**
"I am thinking of building a microsite for [service] in [town, state]. Here is what is on page one when I search it pinned to the town: [paste the ten results]. Using the contest evidence, should I build here? Give me a yes or no first."

**2. Plan the first 30 days**
"I am launching a microsite for [service] in [town]. Plan my first 30 days the way the contest winner ran his, day by day for week one and week by week after that. Mark anything the Academy reports but does not recommend."

**3. Audit my site**
"Here is my site: [address, or paste the page list with word counts]. Go through the pre-launch checks and the facts that separated scoring sites. List the fails first and tell me the three things to fix this week."

**4. Diagnose a drop**
"My site for [search] is [N] weeks old. Positions on my last checks, pinned to the town: [list]. I changed [what] on [date]. What does the contest say I should do?"

**5. Plan my listings**
"My business name is [name], my phone is [number], my town is [town]. Using the winner's list, give me the 25 listings to build first and the order to build them in, then a weekly plan for the rest."

**6. Plan a blog**
"My service pages are [list]. Give me six local blog topics that do not overlap with any service page, in the style of the winner's first six posts, and a schedule for adding more."

**7. Explore the data** (for assistants that can run code)
"Open `data/rankings_oct19_to_nov17.csv`. For each search, show which kinds of site held page one on every check, and which contest sites stayed in the top 20 longest."
