The pattern

  • Profiles ranked first. Listings on trusted sites were on page one eight days after launch. The site took five more weeks to follow them.
  • Each big republish caused a drop, then a higher recovery. Oct 7: down 12 spots, then up to 4. Oct 27: down from 1 to 13, then back to 1 in six days.
  • Both peaks came during quiet periods. It reached number one ten days after its last edit, and has held it with no edits at all.

                                                                      What to copy, and what not to

                                                                      Copy the method

                                                                      • One service, one town, and pages that could only be about that town.
                                                                      • A home page that answers the whole topic and links to every other page.
                                                                      • The money question, the process, and the state law each on their own page.
                                                                      • Posts that each answer one homeowner question, citing the agency or standard by name.
                                                                      • Around 15 matching profiles in launch week, listed in the schema.
                                                                      • Ship in one batch. Expect a dip. Then leave it alone.

                                                                      Do not copy these

                                                                      • The text. It belongs to the contestant who wrote it. Use the structure, write your own.
                                                                      • The identity claims. The site names an owner, shows an office address, and states insurance cover, a one year warranty and round the clock help. Only say those things if they are true for your business.
                                                                      • The scale on day one. It ranked number one at 47 pages. The 119 came after.
