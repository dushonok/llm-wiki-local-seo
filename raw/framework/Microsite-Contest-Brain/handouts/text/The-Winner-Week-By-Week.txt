What to take from it
The first ranking is not the real one. The site touched page one in week three and then slid for two weeks. Nothing was wrong. Google was still deciding.
The slide is where people break their sites. Weeks four and five are when most builders start rewriting pages. This builder made two small updates and waited.
A big update costs you a few days. Going from 47 to 119 pages dropped the site from 1 to 13. It took five days to come back, and it came back stronger. He has since told us the update was an accident.
The reward came from leaving the site alone. The last three weeks at number one had no edits to the site. He has since told us his work in that stretch was all off the site.
