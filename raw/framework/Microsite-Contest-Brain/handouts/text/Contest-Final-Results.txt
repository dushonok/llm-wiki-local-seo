How the winners were decided
Each entered site earns points for its position in the regular Google results on the final check: 50 points for first place, 40 for second, 34 for third, and so on down to 1 point for 20th. Below 20th earns nothing. A contestant's score is the total across their entered sites. Only sites on the official entry sheet, registered on or after September
15 and submitted by September 20, can score.
Rankings were measured in a normal Chrome window with the location set to Gettysburg or York and personalization turned off. Ads, the map box, the AI answer and the forum box were skipped, and each site was counted once at its best page.
What happened on radon
No entered site finished in the top 20 for "radon mitigation york pa", so no radon points were awarded. The closest entered sites were yorkparadonmitigation.net at 30 (Abhishek S.), yorkradonhelp.com at 31 (Tony A.) and radonmitigationnearyorkpa.com at 32 (Nick C.).
The number 1 result for that search on the final check was absoluteradonmitigationllc.com, the site the daily posts called the mystery site. It is run by contestant Blair W. for a real York radon company. It was never on the entry sheet, so it could not score.
All three prizes were won on the mold search.
