Notes
Site figures come from copies saved on Oct 24, 2026, except the mold leader, saved Oct 27 after its last change. Sites that changed after their capture date will be re measured on the final day. Five sites could not be captured and show "not captured". Positions come from pinned crawls between Oct 19 and the date above: Chrome, search pinned to the town, personalization off, organic results only, one position per domain.
The never entered York company is shown for comparison only. It is a real business that was not part of the contest, so its row is analysis and its site is not reproduced anywhere in the contest materials.
Averages describe what the groups looked like. They are not proof of cause. The clearest differences on mold are the number of matching outside profiles and the depth of the home page. On radon, no contest site held a scoring position, so the groups show who got closest.
