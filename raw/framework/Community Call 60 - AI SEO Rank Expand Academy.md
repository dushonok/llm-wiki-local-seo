💬 Discussion

Blair's story set the tone for the call. The video-heavy entries in the microsite contest pushed him to automate instead of leaning harder on his team, and that sparked a bigger talk about removing the single machine bottleneck: VPS droplets, multiple laptops, even a dedicated Mac, all just to run more AI instances at once.

On the Google Business Profile side, recency and frequency keep beating raw review count. Steve is already seeing leads climb before most of his 51 profiles show up in the map pack, and he's finding an exact or partial match name can outrank a profile with around 20 reviews. Once a listing is actually making money, a cheap LLC or DBA is worth it to protect it if it gets flagged.

On the money side, the room landed on starting commission, graduating to flat fee. The formula that came up: aim to put about four dollars of net profit in a client's pocket for every dollar they send you, gather a few months of data, then offer the flat rate once there's trust and proof. Ethan's junk removal deal is a good example of timing it right.

There's also a contest rematch coming, and a jargon glossary the group started crowdsourcing live for anyone newer who's drowning in acronyms.
