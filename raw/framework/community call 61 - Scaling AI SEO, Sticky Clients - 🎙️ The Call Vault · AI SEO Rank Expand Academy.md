## What we covered

Teri's question on repetitive, spammy microsites led to the core idea: Claude needs real context, not just the build file. Brian's 8-month knowledge base got an automotive locksmith site 5 leads before the sitemap was submitted.

Shlomi's part-time retainer question led to pricing deliverables, not results, and routing leads through your own CRM so clients come to rely on your system. Nick asked about monetizing directories: one member calls businesses right after they submit a free listing and sells a featured placement, and directories were pitched as an AI trust play.

Robert shared a Corvette referral site ChatGPT built in about 20 minutes and his mobile home repair sites, Mark has 6 leads from new Christmas light sites in Canada, and Steve's GBP factory raised the service-area versus address-shown question. An affiliate site got a reality check: local SEO is still the easier climb.
