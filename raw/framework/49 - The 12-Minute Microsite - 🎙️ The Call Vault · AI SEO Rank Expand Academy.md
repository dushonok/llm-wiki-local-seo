#49 - The 12-Minute Microsite

57:38

The heart of this one was Mark Earle walking through his build, from buying a domain to a live site with a phone number and a voice agent in about twelve minutes. We also got into the AI watermarking talk, the affiliate side of things, and how you'd feed a hundred domains to Claude.

- SiteGround GoGeek and Cloudflare Pages, for hosting static sites without hitting size limits.
- Omega Indexer, to bulk-submit every URL for indexing.

## What we covered

Jesse opened with a request to keep the community human: no AI-written comments, just talk to each other, and if you want to go faster, dictate with Wispr Flow. Then a long thread, sparked by the news that Anthropic will watermark Claude's output for EU compliance. The read from the room was mostly a nothing burger, since too much of the internet is already AI to ban it, and any enforcement is more likely to come through ad networks like Raptive and Mediavine than from Google. Brandon shared how he keeps content under 1% on the detectors (Pangram is the one to beat) with a detailed rules doc and real research fed in, and William walked through why a watermark could be far harder to strip than people assume.

The affiliate side is still paying. Blair's affiliate site does five to six thousand a month on eighty-seven thousand pageviews with no display ads, all Amazon and AvantLink, and he even uses it to build links to his clients. Sandra's six-week-old travel-card site surprised her with a two hundred dollar check, and she's buying more domains to stack them.

Then Mark. He is at eighty-eight live sites since early June and can stand one up in about twelve minutes. Manus builds the static HTML, he uploads the zip to SiteGround, Omega indexes the URLs, LoopNet fills the address, Nano Banana keeps the images unique, and Retell puts a voice agent on the phone in thirty seconds. It costs a couple of dollars a site plus the domain, and one of his sites pulled eighteen calls in a day. His discipline is to research first and put out three to five a day rather than buy a hundred domains blind.

That led into how you'd actually build a hundred at once: a master Git branch of templates that deploys to Cloudflare, or a Hermes agent connected to DataForSEO that you drive from Telegram. Brandon closed a loop with a warning worth repeating, that mortgage and lending microsites are off limits unless you're properly licensed, because the fines run into the millions.
