Microsite Pitch Example For New Clients

One of my clients just received this email and I thought they did a really good job on the email and their offer website.

Email:

Comment or Message

Hi,

There is already a live painting website for Savannah that we rent to one local painter. Your branding goes on it, and the homeowner leads come to you. It is $20 for the first month.

Want me to send a four-minute walkthrough? You can reach me at [leads@fastjobleads.com](http://mailto:leads@fastjobleads.com/ "http://mailto:leads@fastjobleads.com").

Don Lawrie, Fast Job Leads

If this isn't for you, just reply "no" and I won't reach out again.

Their Website:

[https://fastjobleads.com/](https://fastjobleads.com/ "https://fastjobleads.com/")
