Small moves > no moves

The other day I gave my law firm's website a 10-min facelift to improve the "what we do - who we serve" and help me stand out. Payoff - got a new lead of exactly my ideal client. Clarity attracts (and repels).

I had not changed the copy since I started in 2014. 😱 I started planning this almost a year ago. I ran an exercise that combed the internet for "about me" and my writing voice and produced some great recommendations and suggested a massive new beautiful website. That is what ended up on the shelf - too big, do it later. The whole-house rebuild just felt like too big a renovation project and it sat there in the "Idea - Jar" (someday - maybe).

After getting a new inquiry for work I don't want to do (unqual lead = waste my time), I realized the old copy was wasting time by attracting work I didn't want. Must do something!

I made the reno project much smaller. What is the smallest possible rewrite I can do that points me in the direction I want to grow? I'm (more than) at capacity and am building my "No" muscle. Future-me was pleased.

As an "accidental entrepreneur" the first iteration felt like fiction since I was inventing everything. ("make it up, make it happen!") The 2026 version of me asked, what business do I want to be in? Is the website selling that? What is uniquely me?

I re-wrote from the POV of what does my visitor already know? Less explaining. We (Claude & I) kept the text short and tight. I drove. Claude did the actual work in my Squarespace account - typing and formatting. (so weird to watch Claude work through my browser - kinda like an octopus climbing out of its tank). (Claude's a neurotic proofreader - "you know, boss, you have an extra space in front of two hypens on your bullet points? want me to fix it?").

I finished the re-fresh in two one-hour sessions over two days. (I know, longer than 10-minutes, but still that white lie helped me start and kept me from going down a rabbit hole).

And yesterday got a "cold" inquiry of exactly the work I want to grow (refund recovery claims in Court of Federal Claims)!

my procrastination fix -> make it smaller. small moves > no moves

hope this helps.
