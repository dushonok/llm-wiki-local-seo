## 🧱 Core Topics Discussed — Week 13

## ✅ Hiring, Managing & Scaling With VAs

This week kicked off with a deep dive into the most painful—and most transformative—part of scaling: **finding and managing VAs without redoing their work**.

**Key takeaways:**

- Hiring great VAs is *tedious* for everyone. Even top performers went through **8–12+ failed hires** before finding “the one.”
- The winning method:
	- Hire **3–5 VAs at once**
		- Give them all the same task
		- Keep 1, fire the rest  
		→ 5 weeks of hiring gets compressed into *5 days.*

**What works in the real world:**

- Philippines VAs: $90–$150/week
- India VAs: great generalists, but design eye varies
- “Utility player” VAs change your life forever once found.

### Advanced Insight

If you want scalable VAs, you MUST build systems like manufacturing:

**PDCA Framework**

- **Plan** → Create the SOP
- **Do** → VA executes it
- **Check** → Measure the output
- **Act** → Fix the gaps

Your output is only as good as your SOP.  
If people keep failing → **your training failed, not the VA.**

**How detailed should SOPs be?**

- Brent’s take: extremely detailed, “no mental decisions,” foolproof.
- Shawn’s take: depends on the system — too detailed = people can stop thinking.
- Community consensus:  
	→ SOPs should be detailed enough for repeatability, but not so rigid you remove all autonomy.

## ✅ What Is a “Microsite” — The Official Definition

Community-wide definition:

### ✔ A microsite is:

- A **fast**, **cheap**, **highly focused**, **50+ page site**
- Built with a repeatable templated system
- Designed to rank for one main service + its locations
- Without “big website” overhead or complexity

### ✔ Why the term matters:

Talk about “microsites” online →  
**We become the only people who rank for the term.**

Semantic ownership.

## ✅ Internal Linking & Semantic Branching for Service Businesses

This became a **huge topic** — especially around how many service pages to interlink.

### The Rank Expand Framework:

**Two silos:**

1. **Location pages**
2. **Service pages**

**Rules:**

- Service ↔ Location interlinking
- Sibling services only interlink if semantically connected
- Don't link 10 services to each other “just because”
- Link inside the content where relevant  
	→ If it’s not mentioned, **don’t link it**

**Avoid muddying the waters.**  
Keep the site clean, tight, and semantically aligned.

### Advanced insight (from Brent):

Your website must mirror your **GBP knowledge graph.**

If you link or add services **outside of your GBP category**, you dilute the site.

## ✅ When to Use Multi-Service Sites vs Many Single-Service Microsites

Two competing models came up:

### Model 1: Rank Expand Micro Site

- 1 primary service
- 1 main location
- 20–50 city pages
- 3–5 supporting services max  
	→ Lean, fast, rankable.

### Model 2: Full “Deep Service” Website

(Blair’s approach from traditional SEO clients)

- 10–20 service pages
- Interlinked to all locations dynamically
- Can work extremely well  
	→ but heavy, slower, riskier for scaling 100 sites

**Micro sites = fast money + minimal maintenance.**  
**Full-site builds = long-term authority + GBP alignment.**
