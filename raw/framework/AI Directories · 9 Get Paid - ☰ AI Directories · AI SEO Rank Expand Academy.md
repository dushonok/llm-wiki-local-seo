#### Your directory is live and Google is finding it. Now it earns.

Three ways, and you can run all three. Start with the first one, because it works from day one and it needs nothing built.

## 1\. Make the calls

Every business on the site is a business you now have a reason to call. The line is simple:

> *"We're connected because you have a listing on my directory, the largest directory of \[service\] in \[state\]. Is your listing correct?"*

Then: *"It isn't optimized yet. No photos, no links. Want me to fix that?"* From there it is the conversation you would have had anyway: the listing, the website, the SEO. One of our members says directories are his number one source of SEO clients, and it is the call that does it, not the site.

## 2\. Handle claims

Every business page has "Is this your business? Claim it." Claiming is free. When a claim comes in:

1. Check it is real. A local business, not a lead-gen site. The email domain matches their website. The phone matches the listing.
2. Call them the same day. "Saw you claimed your listing, welcome." That is the warmest lead you will ever get.
3. Tell Claude: `Mark <business> as claimed.` Run Gate 5, upload.

There are no logins. Nobody edits their own listing. Every change goes through you, which is what keeps the spam out.

## 3\. Sell the spots

Three products, all sold by hand. You send a Stripe Payment Link, they pay, you flip the row, you rebuild.

- **Premium listing, $97 a year.** The optimized listing: their photos, all their links, the map, their hours, and a "Listed on \[your directory\]" badge they put on their own site. The badge links back to their page. That is the easiest sale on the list.
- **Best-of placement, one time.** Every city page is already a "best \[service\] in \[city\]" list. Pin a business to a top spot, package it with a press release, and charge for the package. Members have sold this for $750 to $1,200. Call the businesses on the list first to congratulate them. Six months later, call the ones who are not on it.
- **Featured slots, monthly.** The top card on a city page, a group of cities, or the whole state. $29, $99 and $499 a month are the starting points. One business per slot per city.

To set them up: create the Payment Links in Stripe (a free account is enough), paste them into the featured tiers file in the Blueprint, run Gate 5. After a sale, tell Claude which business bought which product, run Gate 5, upload.

**Later: sell the leads.** Once city pages rank, a tracked number or lead form on a city page turns it into a rank-and-rent asset. That is the Microsite Playbook's Step 4 applied here, and it is not a first-month move.

## Your own microsites

If you own sites in this vertical, they are in the directory like any other business. Same card, same page, no link between the sites, no badge. The boost comes from owning the results page three ways, not from linking your sites together.

**Do this now:** make ten calls off the city page for your biggest city. Post what happened in the community.
