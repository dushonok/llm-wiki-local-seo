Ready to build a strong foundation for search visibility? Technical SEO is essential to ensure search engines can crawl, index, and understand your site—making it a core pillar of long-term organic growth.

This lesson brings together expert-vetted guides, free tools, and practical tips to help you master key technical SEO concepts—from crawling and indexing to site speed, structured data, and Core Web Vitals. Whether you’re running your first audit or tackling complex technical challenges, you’ll find trusted resources from leaders like Google, Moz, Ahrefs, and Screaming Frog.

Without solid technical SEO, even the best content can go unnoticed. These curated materials cover everything from robots.txt and XML sitemaps to schema markup and mobile optimization—equipping you with the knowledge and tools to build a search-friendly site from the ground up.
