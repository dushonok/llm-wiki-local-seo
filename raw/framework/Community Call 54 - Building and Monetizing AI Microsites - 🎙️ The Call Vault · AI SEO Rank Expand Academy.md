54 - Building and Monetizing AI Microsites

56:31

The tools make building nearly free, so this call was about the next lever: directories as a way to get in front of every local business in a niche, and the ways members are turning listings and sites into paid relationships.

## Things mentioned

- Buzz, software for running your Claude subscription on your own machine from anywhere, with granular permissions and shared agents ([https://buzz.xyz/](https://buzz.xyz/ "https://buzz.xyz/")).
- Replit, for hosting sites and managing them with its built-in agent ([https://replit.com/](https://replit.com/ "https://replit.com/")).
- Astro, the static framework several members build on ([https://astro.build/](https://astro.build/ "https://astro.build/")).
- Outscraper, for bulk business-data and social scraping ([https://outscraper.com/](https://outscraper.com/ "https://outscraper.com/")).
- NovaMira, for directory content automation ([https://novamira.ai/pricing/](https://novamira.ai/pricing/ "https://novamira.ai/pricing/")).
- GrokBot, the [x.ai](http://x.ai/ "http://x.ai") agent members are testing for scraping and site edits ([https://x.ai/bot](https://x.ai/bot "https://x.ai/bot")).
- Podstack, for spinning up a business podcast to feature clients ([https://podstack.ai/](https://podstack.ai/ "https://podstack.ai/")).
- GoHighLevel snapshots, the outreach/CRM/automation packages agency sellers use ([https://www.google.com/search?q=ghl+snapshot](https://www.google.com/search?q=ghl+snapshot "https://www.google.com/search?q=ghl+snapshot")).
- Next Tier SEO, shared in the chat ([https://nexttierseo.com/#work](https://nexttierseo.com/#work "https://nexttierseo.com/#work")).
- Also named: WordPress + the WP Directory plugin, Cloudflare hosting, the Microsite Blueprint, DataForSEO, Trust Analytica (paid top-10 listings), and AB Newswire (cheap press releases).

## What we covered

The center of the call was directories. The goal is the same one that produced the microsite model: the lowest-cost site that still works and looks great, which for microsites meant static HTML on Cloudflare. The open question is whether directories can be built the same lean way, given that they need logins, spam protection, and constant content refreshing. WordPress with a directory plugin is the proven path when you have VAs, but the team is testing whether Claude can act as the VA and edit sites directly.

Then the room got into money. The old best-of-list trick is still closing deals: list the businesses, call to congratulate the ones who made the list, then months later call the ones who did not and charge them to get on, packaged with a badge, a press release, and a podcast feature. Premium listings run around a hundred dollars a year, single placements have gone for over a thousand, and a paid top-ten listing through a service ran a member seven hundred and fifty.

Under all of it was the same truth that keeps surfacing: cold outreach still does the work. Once you scrape a directory you see that almost every business owner needs what we do, so the whole game is giving them a reason to trust you. One member has his eighty-two-year-old father running the pitch off a directory and pulling in audit requests every day.

We closed on the tooling behind running this at scale, the scraping and agent tools, and a longer thread on buying local hardware and running Claude locally so the cloud bills do not run away.
