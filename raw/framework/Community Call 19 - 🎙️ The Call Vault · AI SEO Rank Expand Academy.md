**HeyGen**  
AI avatar video generator that turns scripts into talking-head videos. Used to scale YouTube, Shorts, and news-style content without recording yourself. B

Google’s AI image generation and transformation tool. Used to fix VA image mistakes, generate alternate versions of images, and align visuals with article content.

AI aggregation dashboard that combines multiple AI tools in one interface. Used to give VAs controlled access to image generation and editing without exposing main accounts.

- Tier 3 = Web 2.0 factories (Neo-style)

## 🗺️ Google Business Profile (GBP) Leverage

**Posts That Actually Matter**

- Use **Offers** or **Events**, not standard posts
- Offers can stay live up to 1 year
- Include 3 links in description:
	1. CID link
		2. Previous post link
		3. Service page link

**Review Reply Strategy**

- Always include:
	- Business name
		- Location
		- Service performed
- Replies are written for *future readers*, not the reviewer
- Negative reviews = credibility boost if handled professionally
