Email Address?

You don’t need to pay for Google Workspace just to receive email.

When you register a domain through Cloudflare, you can use their free Email Routing service to create addresses like info@DanielBrandon.com and forward incoming messages to your regular email.

Cloudflare doesn’t provide a traditional email inbox or outgoing email service with this feature, but there are a couple of ways to configure sending from your domain if you want to do that too.

One downside I’ve noticed is that once I publish an email address on a new website, the spam starts rolling in. Most of it comes from people claiming they’ve discovered problems with my website and offering to send a free report.

I used to respond out of curiosity, but they usually send attachments, which I won’t open, so now I just mark them as spam.

Google Search Console?

I added my first three websites to Google Search Console, but then stopped.

My concern was that if Google identified a problem with one of my sites, having all of them associated with the same account might make it easier for Google to de-index all of them.

Workers on or off?

I leave Cloudflare Workers turned on, because I want to use Workers for my sites instead of Pages. That’s what Cloudflare now recommends for new projects.

At the top of Cloudflare’s Pages documentation, they say:

“Are you sure you want to use Pages?

Workers supports most Pages use cases and offers a broader feature set. It is Cloudflare’s primary platform for building applications. Start new projects with Workers.”

[https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/ "https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/")

and

[https://blog.cloudflare.com/pages-and-workers-are-converging-into-one-experience/](https://blog.cloudflare.com/pages-and-workers-are-converging-into-one-experience/ "https://blog.cloudflare.com/pages-and-workers-are-converging-into-one-experience/")

Hope this helps!

I'm sure Google doesn't need us to use GSC to link our websites, but I don't want to make it too easy for them :)

If I had to guess, they can probably link them in many different ways: Websites might share the same Google Analytics tracking ID, Google Tag Manager container, Google Ads account, phone numbers, email addresses, or business information. Google could also identify connections through privacy policies, structured data, publicly available domain registration information, websites linking to each other, similar backlink patterns, identical images, shared assets, and similarities in HTML, CSS, templates, and page layouts. Even hosting information might give them clues.

Personally, I'd prefer that Google didn't associate all my websites with one another, but I don't lose sleep over it. I figure the best protection is to do things the right way and avoid giving Google a legitimate reason to penalize them.
