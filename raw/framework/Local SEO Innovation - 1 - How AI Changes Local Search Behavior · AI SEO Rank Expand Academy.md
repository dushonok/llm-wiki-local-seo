**Goal:** Understand how AI Overviews reshape local intent, visibility, and clicks.

### **The Shift: What’s Really Changing**

- AI Overviews now appear above the map pack at times stealing clicks before users scroll.
- Local intent queries (“roofers near me,” “best plumber in DC”) can trigger AI summaries showing 3–5 local businesses.
- Fewer clicks → more zero-click conversions (calls, directions, messages straight from SERP).
- Reviews and sentiment are being summarized, not just listed.
- AI evaluates context, not just keywords (your entity strength, trust signals, and topical authority now matter more.)

### **How AI Alters Local Intent**

- Users ask questions, not keywords:  
	→ “Who’s the most trusted roofer near me?” vs “roofers Baltimore MD”
- AI interprets trust and authority from reviews, citations, and consistency.
- Searcher behavior shifts from “browse and compare” → “trust and act.”
- Local search becomes conversational — more about recommendations than rankings.

### **What Happens to Map Packs and Reviews**

- Map Packs shrink or move lower. AI Overview pre-filters which 2–3 listings appear.
- Star ratings are less visible. Review language and sentiment are quoted instead.
- Volume still matters, but now velocity (recent reviews) and context (mentions of service/location) carry extra weight.
- Businesses with rich, structured data and review freshness dominate the AI layer.

### **How to Appear in AI-Summarized Local Results**

- Optimize your Google Business Profile like a “data source,” not a brochure.
- Build review velocity + sentiment (recent, descriptive, location-rich feedback.)
- Add LocalBusiness + Service schema to all location pages
- Publish FAQ and proof content AI can cite (short, factual, scannable).
- Ensure NAP consistency across every citation.
- Strengthen entity connections (mentions in local news, directories, associations).
