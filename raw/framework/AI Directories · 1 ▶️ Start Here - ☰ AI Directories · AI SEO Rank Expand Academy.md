#### This is the build-first path to a directory that earns three ways

A microsite is one service in one town. A directory is every business in one service across one state. Same idea, larger scale: "pool builders near me," "pool builders in Florida," "pool builders in Orlando." One site, a page for the state, a page for every city, a page for every real business.

You build it from a Google Maps listings pull and a Blueprint you hand to Claude Code. There is no WordPress, no plugin, no database. It is a folder of files on free hosting, and when something changes you rebuild the folder. Every directory built this way has the same structure and page set, taken from one master template we tested on a real state. The look comes from a website you name.

## Why build one

1. **It gives you a reason to call every business in the niche.** "We're connected because you have a listing on my directory. Is your listing correct?" That call is the warmest cold call in local SEO, and for most members it is worth more than anything the site earns on its own.
2. **It boosts a vertical you already own.** Microsites in three cities plus one directory for the state means you hold the results page three ways. Your own sites go in the directory like any other business. No links between them.
3. **It rents its top spots.** Premium listings, best-of placements, featured slots. Small numbers each, and they add up because there are a lot of businesses.

## The whole path, in order

1. Pick your vertical and location
2. Buy your domain
3. Pull your listings
4. Enrich your listings
5. Build your directory
6. Put it online
7. Get it indexed
8. Get paid

Your directory is live at Step 6. Everything after that is making it earn.

## What you need before you start

- Claude Code, on the Claude subscription you already have. If you have not set it up, the Setup Microsite Blueprint lesson in Microsite Empire walks through the install.
- DataForSEO connected to Claude Code, if you want the cheap listings pull in Step 3. The Gear Up lesson Connect DataForSEO to Claude does it in one command.
- Node.js on your machine. The Blueprint builds the site with it in Step 5. Free, from [nodejs.org](http://nodejs.org/ "http://nodejs.org"), version 20 or newer.

Everything else on this path is a website you sign up for.

## What it costs

About $10 a year for the domain. The listings pull is a few dollars on DataForSEO, or $25 to $120 on Outscraper depending on the size of your state. Hosting is free. Indexing is about $60 of credits. Call it under $100 the first time with DataForSEO, under $200 with Outscraper, then $10 a year.

The heavy part is not money, it is Claude. Reading every business's website and writing every city page runs on your Claude plan, and a big state can use most of it for a day or two. Claude tells you the size before it starts, and a small state is a fraction of that.

For the same money you could build ten microsites. So build a directory when you already own the vertical, or when you want the call list. Not as your first site.

## How long it takes

The build is a weekend, and most of that is Claude working while you wait. Ranking is the same curve as a microsite: indexed in month one, Google makes up its mind by month three, trusted from month six. One member rebuilt a directory this way and went from 166 indexed pages to 887 in about 30 days, with the first leads and the first business asking to be listed in the same window. That is the shape to expect, not a promise.

**Do these in order.** Nothing else on this page matters yet.
