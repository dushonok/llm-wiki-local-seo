1st lead from directory

Alright, I’m pretty stoked. I just hopped off a call with a company that responded to my cold email last week about my directory listing.

I asked them to update their profile, and the only response I got was: "Alright, that’s okay."

Normally, my usual response would be to email them and tell them about the featured options.

But this time I took my phone out and called them right away. (This is something I’m scared off but I thought f$@k it).

So on the call, I introduced myself and wanted to share the featured options with the guy. And his response was, "If it costs me money, then we are finished”. So I asked more and more questions, and ultimately I brought the minisites into the discussion.

Now I got his attention; he asked what about hosting, maintenance, SEO, web design, etc. And I said all-in for €100 a month per site. He said he had 10 extra domains and asked if we could do all this per site it would be €1000 per month. “That’s super cheap”, he responded. Almost a no-brainer.

He would think about it this weekend, but I hope on Monday I will have a € 1000-a-month SEO deal.

A great way to start the weekend. Now fingers crossed for Monday.
