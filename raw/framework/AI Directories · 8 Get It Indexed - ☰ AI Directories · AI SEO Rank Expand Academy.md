#### Google does not know your site exists yet. This tells it, and it tells it the right amount.

A directory launches with hundreds or thousands of pages. Push them all at once and Google indexes a few and ignores the rest. So the Blueprint launched lean: the home page, the state page, every city and town page and the strong business pages are in the sitemap, and the thin business pages (under five reviews, or no description) are built but marked not to index. They still show, they still link, they still say "claim this listing." They join the index once they earn it. On the state we tested, that was about 1,100 pages in the sitemap out of 1,700 built.

1. **Step 1. Get your list of URLs.** Claude wrote them in order for you at Gate 6, in `06-launch.md`: strong pages first. Or open `yourdomain.com/sitemap-index.xml`, open the sitemap it points to, and copy every URL. That is the launch set. Do not start until Claude's live check from the last step passed.  
	  
	Every address is one credit, so a big launch adds up. To spend less up front, ask Claude: What will indexing cost, and can we put the most important pages first? It splits the list into tiers: the home, state and city pages, then the strongest listings, then the rest. On the state we tested the first two tiers were 584 addresses and about $44, against about $81 for all of it. The rest waits for the 30-day check.
2. **Step 2. Create the campaign in Omega.** **Enter Dashboard → Campaigns → + Create Campaign → Copy & Paste Links.** Paste the list into the field under "Links." No file upload.
3. **Step 3. Set the dripfeed to 7 to 10 days.** A directory launch is a big build. Do not run it in a day.
4. **Step 4. Launch the campaign.** Then leave it alone.
	**On Omega's pricing**: buy a subscription, cancel it, and the credits stay. One credit is one address. The plan we used was 800 credits for $60, so 1,000 addresses is about $75. On the state we tested, the first two tiers fit inside one purchase.e subscription. Credits stay good for months. A 1,000-page launch uses most of them.
5. **Step 5. The 30-day check.** Write the date down. In 30 days look at two numbers: how many pages Omega reports indexed, and how many visitors Cloudflare's analytics shows. If both are moving, tell Claude: `Widen the sitemap.` It flips the next set of business pages to index, claimed ones first, then by review count. Run Gate 5, upload, paste the new URLs into a new Omega campaign. Repeat every month while the numbers grow.

**No Search Console on this site.** Same reason as the microsites: a directory that sits next to your microsites should not share a Google account with them. Omega's report and Cloudflare's analytics are enough.

> **Please note**: you are free to use Google Search Console if that's what you want to do.

**Do this now:** paste the sitemap into Omega, set 7 to 10 days, launch. Put the 30-day check in your calendar.
