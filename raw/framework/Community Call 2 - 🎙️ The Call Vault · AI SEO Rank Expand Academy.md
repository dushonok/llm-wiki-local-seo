1:02:39

### SEO / CTR Manipulation Tools

- **CTR Booster** – Swiss Army knife CTR manipulation tool, simulates user journeys.
- **Kraken** – Advanced CTR tool
- **Assassin** – High-end CTR tool, expensive, used for manipulation at scale.

### Link Building / Cloud Tools

- **SearchAtlas** – Cloud linking platform (noted as buggy and slow, “had potential”).
- **QGP Links** – Mentioned, but nobody on the call seemed to use or trust it.

### Hosting / Phone / Utilities

- **Grasshopper** – Cheap business phone numbers ($5/line), used for microsite/lead gen setup.
- **XnConvert** – Free tool for stripping metadata (EXIF/geo) from images.

### Content / Image Tools

- **MidJourney** – AI image generator, used for creating unique variants across sites.
- **Ideogram** – AI image generator that spits out multiple variations at once.
- **Canva** – Used for overlays (logos, titles) to make AI blog images look unique/brandable.

### Review / Social Proof Tools

- **ProvenExpert** – Collects and displays reviews/testimonials.
- **Facebook Reviews + GHL Review Widget** – Pulls FB & Google reviews into sites via GoHighLevel widget.

### SEO Tactics / Data Tools

- **OutScraper** – Used to find local businesses and build contact lists.
- **LoopNet** – Commercial real estate site leveraged for finding addresses for NAP/Google Maps.
