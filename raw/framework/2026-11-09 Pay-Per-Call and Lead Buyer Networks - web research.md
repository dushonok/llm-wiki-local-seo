# Pay-Per-Call / Lead Buyer Networks — Web Research Capture (2026-11-09)

Captured by Claude (web research, 2026-11-09) at the user's request: "5 most promising
platforms that buy leads/calls, excluding Polyares and Marketcall" (user already uses
https://www.polyares.com/ and has started registering at https://www.marketcall.com/).
Notes below are paraphrased findings + source URLs, not verbatim copies of the pages.

## Lead Smart Inc
- https://leadsmartinc.com/news/press-offervault-top-pay-per-call-network-2026 — US home-services PPCall + lead-gen network since 2008; 10,000+ registered publishers; buyers cover ~50,000 home-service locations, 200+ categories; two platforms (in-house dashboard with one number for 200+ services, and a Ringba-powered platform with per-service offers at higher payouts); explicitly courts local SEO specialists and rank-and-rent operators; apply at leadsmartinc.com/affiliates; top-3 in every OfferVault PPCall survey since 2020.
- https://leadsmartinc.com/news/press-google-lsa-lead-costs-rise-payout-data-september-2026 — published median payouts (5 Sep 2026): mold removal (CPL call) $65.50 median / $159 highest; plumbing CPL $48; plumbing pay-per-call $33.25 median / $221.95 highest; water damage $213 median; CPL pays on qualified lead/appointment, PPCall pays on call duration.
- https://leadsmartinc.com/news/press-blue-book-2026-rankings-lead-smart-fifth — ranked #5 PPCall network in mThink 2026 Blue Book (top five: Aragon Advertising, CJ, Marketcall, GlobalWide Media, Lead Smart); full ZIP-level payout file public, no login: https://affiliate-bid-coverage.leadsmartinc.com/
- https://www.leadsmartinc.com/news/press-public-payout-file-pay-per-call-transparency-2026 — home-service-only median $34.38 (20 Oct 2026 extract).
- https://www.skool.com/pay-per-call-academy-6436/cpl-payout-sheet-for-ringba-offers — pays by 2nd Monday of month; ACH (Bill.com), Wise or PayPal. Free "Pay Per Call Academy" Skool community (17 free courses incl. Rank and Rent, Local SEO).
- https://www.skool.com/pay-per-call-academy-6436/new-international-opportunity-limited-access-canada-water-damage — limited Canada test (water damage / emergency home services) for affiliates with Canadian sites or GBPs.

## Service Direct
- https://blog.servicedirect.com/transform-consumer-calls-into-revenue-with-easy-earn — Austin TX, since 2006; Affiliate Program (pay-per-call + pay-per-lead) selling to US local service businesses; "Easy Earn" = static DID number, no coding; reported 90%+ buyer-found rate and 68% payout rate; must apply and qualify. Program page: https://servicedirect.com/pay-per-lead-affiliate-program/
- https://blog.servicedirect.com/what-to-expect-selling-calls-to-service-direct — calls human-scored on dozens of criteria; key KPIs: Service Needed Rate (wrong numbers, solicitations, service-not-offered count against you) and Buyer Dispute & Scratch Rate; affiliates failing KPIs can be terminated; payouts rise with call quality. No published rates/payment terms.
- https://blog.servicedirect.com/unlock-key-insights-to-drive-better-outcomes-with-affiliate-insights-platform — Affiliate Insights Platform (Nov 2024) tags calls by service-need / wrong number / spam.

## eLocal
- https://elocal.com/affiliate-contact — since 2008; PPCall/PPL in 50+ verticals (home services, legal, insurance, etc.); every call screened by IVR or in-house call center for category + geo before forwarding; weekly payouts for affiliates meeting a minimum monthly volume; BBB A+.
- https://www.offervault.com/affiliate-networks/profile/networkId/13110/elocal — accepts only experienced affiliates with existing pay-per-call campaigns, high volume.
- https://www.ringba.com/blog/the-pay-per-call-report-october-8-2021 — eLocal ran a "Mold Removal | 24/7 | ZIP targeting | experience required" offer.

## The Client Connector
- https://theclientconnector.com/HomeServicesPayPerCallAffiliateProgram — home-services live PPCall network, Las Vegas NV, BBB A+; integrated with 20+ PPCall networks + hundreds of direct buyers; 60+ industries incl. plumbing, locksmith, mold removal, mold testing; affiliate payout typically 50–90% of buyer's call price; paid via Tipalti (first month on the 1st and 15th, then monthly); buyers may credit calls up to 3 days after; preferred traffic = SEO/organic and PPC; calls sourced from Google Business Profiles, Craigslist, FB Marketplace and other free sources accepted but get much more limited distribution; must be TCPA compliant; geo not stated (US address).

## CallScaler Marketplace
- https://callscaler.com/docs/pay-per-call — free extension on any CallScaler (call-tracking) plan; "Sell Calls" side lets you browse/apply to public PPCall offers at callscaler.com/marketplace; buyers can also buy directly.
- https://v3.callscaler.com/marketplace/offers/stair-builders — most offers pay ~$12–$85 per qualified call; most publishers approved within 24–48h.
- https://callscaler.com/docs/ppc-how-it-works — call qualifies by minimum length; buyer disputes can reverse; publisher payouts settled monthly via PayPal (network-admin plan).
- https://callscaler.com/marketplace/drain-repair — drain repair typical payout $20–$55.
- https://callscaler.com/docs/ppc-buying-calls — public payout-by-ZIP coverage page: callscaler.com/marketplace/pay-per-call-coverage
- Also useful the other way round: lets you sell calls directly to a local buyer you recruit (own network/buyer), not only through networks.
