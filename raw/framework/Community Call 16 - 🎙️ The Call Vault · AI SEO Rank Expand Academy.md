## 📧 Cold Outreach: What’s Still Working (and What to Avoid)

[**Instantly.ai**](http://instantly.ai/ "http://Instantly.ai") **\+ Google Workspace**

- Still working despite November Gmail crackdown
- Deliverability slightly worse (more pauses, more bounces), but usable
- Not enough degradation yet to justify switching stacks
- If needed, Microsoft 365 is the fallback

**Key principle**

- Never use your primary or branded domain
- Always use **burner domains** (brand + prefix/suffix if needed)
- Domains are disposable — time is not

**Warm-up debate**

- Manual warm-up vs Instantly warm-up
- Consensus: Instantly is worth it
	- Even at ~50% efficiency, it beats manual labor
		- Domains are cheap; time is expensive

**Inbox strategy**

- Multiple inboxes per domain to keep sending patterns natural
- 10–20 emails/day per inbox > blasting from one address
- Once one inbox is flagged, the domain is gone anyway — that’s expected

## 🌐 CMS & Footprints at Scale

**Kirby CMS**

- No meaningful footprint concerns at small–mid scale
- 15+ sites is trivial in Google’s eyes
- Google trains on large, common patterns (WordPress themes, etc.)
- Kirby is obscure enough to fly under the radar

**Bottom line**

- Don’t overthink CMS footprints
- Focus on execution and structure, not paranoia

## 🔗 Backlinks for Local SEO

**For micro sites**

- No guest posts
- No expensive links
- Minimal spend by design

**For client / high-ticket sites**

- Citations
- Press releases
- Guest posts (short, direct outreach)
- Local sponsorship links (“sponsors” searches)
- Internal linking does most of the heavy lifting

**Link velocity**

- Check competitors in Ahrefs
- Match or slightly exceed their monthly link acquisition
- Natural > aggressive

**Outreach email (real example)**

- Subject: *Backlink pricing details needed*
- Body: 2–3 sentences max
- Ask price, insertion options, move on

## 🧭 Google Business Profiles (GBP)

**Key insight**

- Reviews are NOT required to rank early
- Lead flow increased ~5x immediately after GBP activation
- Ranking #1 in map pack with **zero reviews**

**Verification strategy**

- Let sites “cook” 3–6 months with citations first
- Avoid video verification by waiting
- Early GBP creation triggers endless verification loops

---

## 📄 Indexing Strategy

**Micro sites**

- No Google Search Console
- No Google Analytics
- Use Omega Indexer if needed
- Track traffic elsewhere

**Client / authority sites**

- Slow rollout
- Test indexing cadence first
- Never overload Google if indexing is slow
- Revise existing content before publishing more

**Golden rule**

- If 80%+ of pages are indexed and ranking → stop touching it
- Over-optimization kills momentum

---

## 📊 Tracking Without Google

**Analytics**

- Self-hosted analytics (Matomo) used instead of GA
- Avoid portfolio-wide Google visibility risks

**Rank tracking**

- AccuRanker for primary keywords
