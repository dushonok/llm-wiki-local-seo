Multi-Tier SEO Distribution Network

I really didn't know how to write all of this out so bear with me.

I first got inspired by a creator who sells an HTML-based “prompt engine” for AI. On the surface it just looks like a simple HTML page, but under the hood it’s basically a prompt framework in disguise.

Here’s how his setup works:

- He gives you an HTML page where you plug in a keyword or topic.
- When you click a button, the page builds out a full, structured prompt stack behind the scenes.
- Those prompts are tuned specifically to make the AI write better

You then copy the generated prompts into his custom GPT / Claude setup and get back 3,000–5,000 word long-form articles that are:

- Structured for SEO
- Readable like a blog post
- Strong enough to publish as-is on Medium, LinkedIn, GitHub, Quora, etc.

In other words, he’s not just “using AI” — he’s engineered the way the AI is prompted so that you consistently get high-quality, long-form content from a single keyword. He still currently using this very successfully.

So I thought why not take that idea and put in on steriods and see what happens. I took that same “keyword → engineered prompts → 3,000–5,000 word articles” machine and bolt it onto my own ecosystem.

The project implements a 4-tier SEO authority network designed to maximize organic traffic and prevent self-competition:

- Tier 1 (Foundation): Medium + LinkedIn articles (story-driven for Medium, business-focused for LinkedIn)
- Tier 2 (Amplification): Content rotates across LinkedIn, Notion, YouTube, and Claude Artifacts
- Tier 3 (Conversion): FREE error checker microsites on .repair and .dev domains ([pdfwontopen.repair](http://pdfwontopen.repair "http://pdfwontopen.repair"), [pdffilecorrupted.repair](http://pdffilecorrupted.repair "http://pdffilecorrupted.repair"), [pdfprintingerror.repair](http://pdfprintingerror.repair "http://pdfprintingerror.repair"), [pdfnotprinting.repair](http://pdfnotprinting.repair "http://pdfnotprinting.repair"), [printer-offline.repair](http://printer-offline.repair "http://printer-offline.repair"), [syntaxfix.dev](http://syntaxfix.dev "http://syntaxfix.dev"), [npm-install-fails.repair](http://npm-install-fails.repair "http://npm-install-fails.repair"))
- Tier 4 (Core Property): [mrgrid.io](http://mrgrid.io "http://mrgrid.io") main site for direct conversions

Additionally, the system includes automated publishing to [Dev.to](http://Dev.to "http://Dev.to"), Hashnode, GitHub, and Reddit, creating a 9-platform distribution network that publishes content daily with platform-specific optimizations.
