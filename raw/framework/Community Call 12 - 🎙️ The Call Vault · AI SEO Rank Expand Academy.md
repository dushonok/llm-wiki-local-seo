## ✅ EMD vs PMD: The Deep-Dive Everyone Needed

A massive discussion kicked off around Exact-Match Domains (EMDs) vs Partial-Match Domains (PMDs) and the *real* differences in performance.

**Consensus Highlights:**

- EMDs still hit faster. You get the “instant alignment” boost when your domain *is* the query.
- PMDs work — but require more time.
- Dominating a major city (e.g., “Baltimore Electricians”) → use both EMDs + PMDs across multiple sites.
- Winning = building more, not obsessing over suffixes.

**Advanced Insight (from Shawn):**

If you authority-build a PMD correctly, Google can eventually treat the search term like it’s your brand name — a long-term compounding advantage.

**Bonus Framework:**  
  
When top-level EMDs are taken → go deeper:

- Add modifiers (“experts,” “pros,” “247,” “certified”)
- Or go sub-niche first

Sub-niches = easier, cheaper, and more wide-open.

## ✅ SERP Analysis: “Can I Actually Rank For This?”

A member asked: *How do I know if a keyword is worth building a microsite for before committing?*

Rapid SERP Audit Checklist (community consensus):

- Are there DA <15 sites in the top 5?
- Are the ranking pages inner pages instead of homepages?
- Are there no strong EMDs?
- Are there thin pages ranking?
- Are page titles/H1s missing the exact keyword?
- Do you see local businesses, not national brands?

Above-the-Fold Test:

- Count LSAs, Google Ads, and Map Pack visibility.
- If organic sits BELOW 3–4 elements → still workable *if* your EMD sits just under ads.

## ✅ Branding on Micro-Sites: Should You Add the Contractor’s Logo?

A member asked whether adding the client’s brand/logo helps conversion.

Real-world results:

- Clients answer calls even if the site isn’t branded to them — and callers reconcile instantly.
- Light-touch branding works best:  
	*“In partnership with ACME Electric”* in the hero.

Why not fully brand?

- If a partnership ends → rebranding becomes a nightmare.
- Keeps sites reusable & prevents footprint issues.

## ✅ GMB for Micro-Sites: Is It Worth Doing 1 Per Site?

Big discussion on GMBBs (Google My Business + Burner Address):

Key points:

- Plan is to do one per winning microsite, not every single site.
- Verifying requires a unique email per GMB → costs can balloon if using Google Workspace.

Money-saving hack from the community:  
Use Cloudflare email routing to create burner inboxes for FREE.

Address strategy:  
Use fictional but plausible addresses inside business parks:  
*Suite 204, Floor 2, Enterprise Center, City.*

It works.

## ✅ Flat Fee vs Rev Share: What’s Working Now

Flat Fee Rental:

- Easy sales
- Good for quick cash
- Tough for long-term scaling (complaints about lead volume)

Revenue Share:

- Warmer relationships
- Higher lifetime value
- More upside per lead/client
- More complex onboarding

Community consensus:  
→ If you want $10K+ months, RevShare is the path.

## ✅ Why Some Micro-Sites Win and Others Don’t

Pattern across 6+ site builders:

- 2 winner
- 2 mediocre
- 2 duds

Variables:

- Location competitiveness
- Sub-niche selection
- EMD availability
- Level of consumer urgency
- Pricing of the service (high ticket vs tire-kicker hell)

Winning pattern:  
  
Build 5 → find 1 winner → build 5 more in the winner’s niche.

## ✅ NAP, Addresses & Phone Numbers for Rank & Rent

How to get addresses for sites you don’t own?

The winning approach:

- Pick a commercial building
- Use a fictional suite number
- Ensure no existing GMB uses it
- Use burner emails
- Use affordable virtual numbers (or avoid phone entirely via AI/SMS)
