I see 2 approaches out there:

1. Driving signals: simulated drives to the business location with GPS data
2. Interactions on the listing: searching a keyword, clicking the profile, asking for directions, calling, viewing photos

Has anyone tested either one?

- Did the profile move? How fast?
- Which one worked better for you?
- Any suspensions or issues?
