[@Amir Ahangari](https://www.skool.com/@a6c9885023614bfda30a2ca229e785b6?g=rank-expand-academy)

I can go all day on this. Its way too long for starters, its hard but try keeping it around 100 words. Lead with their competitors getting the jobs they could be getting. Looks too technical, you're making them think and when you make them have to think about something they dont understand, you lost them. If you go the route you're going, use bullet points like

Heres where you sit on maps when im searching for a roofer

- #29 searching for Roof Repair CoS
- #59 searching for Roof Replacement CoS

"Would you mind?" no, never. more like "If you want to be seen higher on maps simply reply yes and i'll send a personalized video" Something like that, id still probably tweak it though.

Theres so much more, bottom line is, they need to feel that this is an issue, ie. Their Competitors taking more jobs they could have. They need it to be easy, asking would you mind gives them an easy way out. Don't make them think, if they have to interpret what youre trying to say, you lost them. Youre opt out message is stronger than your CTA. Try to get to the point QUICKLY or eaily by making it scannable like using bullet points.

I get cold emails daily for my agency, the ones i find myself reading are the shorter ones, the ones that get to the point quickly, and the ones that are easy to understand

Theres a lot more but hope that helps
