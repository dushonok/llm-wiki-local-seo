## 🧠 Mindset & Execution Insights

### “Where do I start?” Problem

Common across the group.

**What’s working:**

- AI as a *thought partner*, not a content tool
- Weekly priority compression (“what actually matters this week?”)
- Competing against:
	- Yourself (speed + output)
		- Or peers (friendly pressure)

**Standout concepts:**

- **Sloppy success beats perfect paralysis**
- Timers create momentum (20–30 min sprints)
- Buckets > hustle:
	- Health
		- Family
		- Work
		- Relationships
