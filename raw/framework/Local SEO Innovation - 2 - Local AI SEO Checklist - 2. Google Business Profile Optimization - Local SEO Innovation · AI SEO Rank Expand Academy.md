**Objective:** Make your GBP a “data source” for AI summaries.

- ✅ Business description includes key services + location phrases naturally.
- ✅ Every service is listed individually under *Services* tab.
- ✅ Add service area ZIP codes or neighborhoods.
- ✅ Upload 20+ high-quality photos (before/after, team, trucks, etc.).
- ✅ Weekly post schedule (project highlights, reviews, promos).
- ✅ Q&A section pre-filled with common customer questions and answers.
