How I closed a business owner on a free trial

Hey ya'll!

Just wanted to put this in here as a resource. Excuse my look, I'm very sick and had just woken up, but one of my sites got a call and I immediately cold called a business owner to give them the lead for free. I turned my camera on to film about halfway through this process.

He's on a free trial now and next one I send him, I get a commission on. You can watch in the video, but here's my simple 5 step framework for selling to business owners.

1. Get the lead on the phone. I have all the calls come to my phone number, and I answer it as if I'm just a call agent. I know the niches well enough to talk shop, I can qualify the leads, and get all the information that a business owner would ask. Then, I usually tell them that the business owner is currently on a job and that I'm the guy that just take the calls, but I can call him and see if I can get him on to take a look at next steps.

2\. Then it's time to cold call the business owner. I find someone, usually facebook groups are a good place to look, and immediately start calling. Once I get someone on the phone, I explain what I do and how it works, and tell them I have a hot lead on the phone. Lots of them will just tell me to send the lead to them, but that's a huge red flag for me. I want someone who's hungry and ready to get on the phone now.

3\. I call the customer back, tell them I have the business owner on the phone, merge the business owner in, and then MUTE! This is key, I stay on the phone so I can vet the business owner out and see if they're good on the phones, how they handle the customer, etc. I have a long background in sales so I've gotten a pretty decent feel for who's good and who's bad at sales, but you'll find a good pattern with time.

4\. After the call, I IMMEDIATELY call the business owner back. You want to strike while the iron is hot. You just gave them a lead, they closed them, and made them money. I like to thank them for taking the call, and then I try to get to know them a little bit better. At this point, they are almost always willing to talk to me and let me sell them into the next step and the business model, because I just proved it worked. I"ll ask them some qualifying questions about whether or not they're looking to grow, how many jobs they can take on, whether they'd be willing to pay for leads (to avoid tire kickers)

5\. I get them onboarded. I send them a loom video explaining how it works (Hint: If you send them the video and they don't watch it within 3 days, they're probably not serious and you shouldn't work with them) and once they watch it and let me know they have, I connect their contact info in my CRM to their phone number, email, etc.
