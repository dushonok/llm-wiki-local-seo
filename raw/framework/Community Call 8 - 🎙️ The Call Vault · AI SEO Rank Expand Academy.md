## Community Call #8 Notes

**Theme:** Automation, Attribution, & the Next Phase of Microsite Scaling

### 🧱 Core Topics Discussed

✅ **“Celebrity of the Week” Meme Idea** – Weekly theme for community branding & fun engagement (this week: *Bill Murray*; next week: *Nick Cage*; week 3: *Daniel Day-Lewis*).

✅ **Footprint Avoidance 101** –

- Register domains with **large registrars** and enable **domain privacy** to hide WHOIS info.
- Use **Cloudflare WAF (free tier)** to mask IP addresses and hide server origin.
- Avoid **Google Search Console & GA4**; use **Matomo (formerly Piwik)** instead for analytics.
- Don’t reuse assets, colors, or branding between microsites — even one shared image can connect them in Google’s entity graph.

✅ **Persona & E-E-A-T Strategy** –

- Personas add trust signals but are **not mandatory** for ranking.
- Avoid duplicating the same persona or story across multiple microsites — that links entities together.
- Alternatives: feature local team members, friends, or anonymized “staff” pages if desired.
- Remember: personas build *E-E-A-T* for Google, not for human visitors.

✅ **Citations & Indexing Workflows** –

- Always index citations; unindexed = no power.
- **Drip-feed** indexing (≈5/day) instead of blasting hundreds at once.
- Use tools like **prepostseo.com** or similar indexers.
- Employ **spin-text** for citation descriptions to vary language and increase “stickiness.”
- Create **service-based citation sets** (e.g., Roofing, Roof Repair, Gutters) instead of one global list.
- Interlink tier-1 and tier-2 citations contextually (e.g., one citation links to another with a service-specific anchor).

✅ **Microsite Launch Strategy** –

- Launching simultaneously or staggered both work; more data = faster insights.
- Each site is its own entity — **unique phone numbers**, **unique citations**, **no shared analytics**.
- Use different phone numbers (via Twilio, WhatConverts, etc.) even if they all forward to one central system.

✅ **Automation & n8n Discussion** –

- Debate on full microsite automation via **n8n + Kirby CMS** integration.
- Consensus: *possible but dangerous*.
	- Over-automation → low quality + massive footprint.
		- “Human in the loop” remains best practice (review every deploy).
- Ideal balance: automate **tedious** tasks (citations, content refreshes, internal links) but keep **strategy & QA manual**.
- n8n now supports **Folders (since April 2024)** for organizing workflows.

✅ **Quality vs. Scale Philosophy** –

- “Speed with standards” outperforms mass production.
- Quality microsites (5 hours each) > 10,000 AI-spun sites that collapse in a Google slap.
- Human review keeps contextual signals clean.
- The *“Feedback Loop Vision”*: tie rank trackers + performance tools into an automated site-deployment pipeline that expands clusters based on real SERP data.

✅ **Revenue Models & Lead Handling** –

- **Kate’s Win:** Pet-niche microsite produced a $2,000 service lead within 4 days of launch.
- Start with **RevShare** (10%) or **per-lead tracking** (e.g., 3-minute call = qualified lead), then convert to **flat monthly rental** ($1K/month) once stable.
- Focus on **relationship building** first — easier to switch to rental after proving value.

✅ **Service Area Expansion Blueprint** –

- After a microsite succeeds, expand outward by **radius** (5–10 miles) to build a geographic cluster.
- Each new site = separate entity but part of one local “network.”
- Replicate proven content structures, not designs or images.

✅ **Attribution: PPC vs SEO** –

- Use **dedicated landing pages** and **tagging** to isolate traffic.
- If attribution is broken, request site access to fix tracking (e.g., GA4, WhatConverts).
- Frame it as “I’ll give you clarity” to gain full access.
- Once you have conversion keyword data from PPC, use it to build SEO content around proven terms.

✅ **Voice AI & Lead Handling Insights** –

- Voice AI can capture leads 24/7 (e.g., calls at 2–3 AM).
- Analyze call transcripts to identify patterns, missed opportunities, and conversion quality.
- Use insights for copywriting, ad timing, and automation triggers.

✅ **SERP Consolidation Trend** –

- Google is merging intent — broad keywords now absorb granular ones (e.g., “roof repair” vs “roofing company”).
- Solution: build **tight, topic-focused microsites** instead of large bloated websites.
- Keep each site lean: only 4–6 core services, no content bloat.
- Prioritize **content refreshing** (not endless new pages).

### ⚙️ Tools Mentioned

✅ **Kirby CMS** – Core microsite builder.  
✅ **Cloudflare WAF** – Hides origin IP & prevents tracking.  
✅ **Matomo Analytics** – Non-Google analytics alternative.  
✅ **Twilio / WhatConverts** – Unique phone tracking per site.  
✅ **PrePostSEO Indexer** – Manual citation indexing.  
✅ **n8n** – Workflow automation platform (new Folder feature).  
✅ **AccuRanker / GSC (limited use)** – Rank and data checks.  
✅ **Voice AI Platforms** – For call recording, transcription, and quality scoring.

---

### 🧩 Frameworks & Mindsets

✅ **Human-in-the-Loop Automation** – Always review AI output before deployment.  
✅ **Gold Vein Strategy** – When one site hits, *surround it geographically* and dominate that SERP.  
✅ **Speed with Standards** – Build fast but maintain strict quality.  
✅ **E-E-A-T Layering** – Add personas, social proof, and structured data once core content ranks.  
✅ **Feedback Loop Scaling** – Let ranking data trigger new site builds or AB tests automatically.  
✅ **Consolidated Intent Awareness** – Recognize and adapt to Google’s merging of sub-service SERPs.
