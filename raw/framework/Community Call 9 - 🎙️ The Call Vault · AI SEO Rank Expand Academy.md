### 🧱 Core Topics Discussed

✅ **Prospecting & Lead Research with PhantomBuster**

- **Tool:** PhantomBuster
- **Use Case:** Finds *unverified* or *missing* Google Business Profiles (GMBs).
- **Workflow:**
	- Input niche + location.
		- Extract verified/unverified/claimed data.
		- Target non-verified listings for outreach or microsite builds.
- **Concept:** *GMB Gap Mining* — find local businesses with no presence, fill the void with a microsite.

✅ **Data Enrichment & Visitor Identification with Acquisition Air**

- **Creator:** John Limbacher (of SitePop & CTR Phone fame).
- **Tool:** Acquisition Air
- **Function:** Captures visitor identity data (name, email, phone, address) from website traffic using Neilsen’s data network.
- **Cost:** $99/month
- **Output:** Syncs to Google Sheets automatically.
- **Applications:**
	- Retarget site visitors manually (“Hey, saw you checking us out”).
		- Build email lists *without* opt-ins (gray-hat growth).
		- Tie into AI outreach automation.
- **Alternatives:** [Instantly.ai](http://instantly.ai/ "http://Instantly.ai"), [Customers.ai](http://customers.ai/ "http://Customers.ai") (both similar but higher-priced).

✅ **AI Visibility Audits Using SplashDash**

- **Tool:** SplashDash (by Ruan Marino)
- **Purpose:** Reports whether a company appears in AI search results (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.).
- **Pitch Line:** “Has anyone ever told you, ‘I found you on ChatGPT?’”
- **Process:**
	- Run 25–50 AI prompt tests.
		- Export missing appearances.
		- Use Claude/Gemini to generate FAQ content that targets those missed prompts.
- **Result:** AI SEO visibility jumps — great “foot-in-the-door” offer for clients.
- **Cost:** ~$29/month.

✅ **Cold Outreach & Call Openers That Actually Work**

- “Hey, are you guys still open? My AI told me you were closed.” → disarms instantly.
- “Has any of your customers said they found you on ChatGPT?” → triggers curiosity.
- Informal > professional: keep it real when calling contractors.
- **Visual Follow-Up:** Send SplashDash or GeoGrid report after the call — proof of value.

✅ **Seth’s Pay-Per-Job Contractor Model**

- **Business Model:** Replaces pay-per-lead with **pay-per-job (10%)**.
- **Stack:** Microsites + Twilio routing + manual lead calls.
- **Workflow:**
	- Calls homeowner → connects 3 vetted contractors.
		- Each submits a bid + CC’s Seth for accountability.
		- Earns 10% per closed deal (often $30–40K/month).
- **Automation Layer:** Uses n8n + AI transcription → creates instant proposals + job notes.
- **Philosophy:** “Do the unscalable now. Automate later.”

✅ **n8n Workflow Wins – Real Automation in Action**

- **Tool:** n8n
- **Example:** Kate built a workflow to auto-post recipe content from Google Drive → Sheets → Facebook Pages.
- **Goal:** Earn Facebook Creator Bonuses passively.
- **Lesson:** Facebook API setups are painful — but once connected, the automation is nearly zero-maintenance.
- **Mindset:** Don’t overcomplicate n8n; most flows already have auth + structure done. Just plug and trigger.

✅ **AI Debugging & Development Tips**

- **Claude:** Best conversational debugger for code/config issues.
- **Menace:** Harsh, direct AI dev assistant — “You suck. Here’s how to fix it.” (brutally helpful).
- **Kirby CMS:** Core microsite builder; sensitive routing system vs. WordPress’ permalink simplicity.
- **Advice:** Always version-control (GitHub/FTP) & document every change.
- **Greg’s Insight:** When prompting LLMs for troubleshooting, **feed a working file sample** to anchor the output format.

✅ **Affiliate & Monetization Opportunities**

- [**Stay22.com**](http://stay22.com/ "http://Stay22.com")**:** Autoinserts affiliate links into local/travel/recipe pages — “set it and forget it” revenue.
- **Amazon Associates:** Blair’s site hit 66K visitors & $6K/month from affiliate earnings.
	- Reports lag due to delayed order fulfillment.
		- AI SEO + old affiliate models = major revival opportunity.
- **Extra Insight:** Combine AI SEO affiliate sites with **display ad RPM + FB traffic arbitrage** for compounding returns.

✅ **Link Swaps & Expired Domains for Local Boosting**

- Members agreed to initiate “ **ABC link swaps** ” — interlink community microsites for mutual SEO lifts.
- Blair uses **expired domains** to rebuild local blogs and push press releases for clients.
- **Use Case:**
	- Buy expired domains in client areas.
		- Rebuild them with niche/local content.
		- Use them for local PR backlinks + internal clusters.

✅ **Client Retention & Perception Tactics**

- Blair shared his “Disneyland Experience” framework:
	- Overcommunicate with clients.
		- Keep them in the loop with mini updates & retargeting ads.
		- Even average SEO results feel premium with good communication.
