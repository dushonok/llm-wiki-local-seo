## 🔥 Reddit, Wikipedia & Brand Mentions (Advanced)

**Core rule:**  
Reddit ≠ links neccessarily. Reddit = **brand mentions**.

**Jesse’s process:**

- `site:reddit.com "keyword"`
- Identify live conversations
- Brand mention → wait → upvote
- Never use personal accounts

**Why it matters now:**

- UGC feeds AI models directly
- Brand chatter = relevance signal
- Same principle as CTR manipulation — but organic

**Priority order:**

1. Basic SEO (structure wins 90% of battles)
2. PR / Ads / On-page
3. Reddit & social mentions (strong *extra*, not base)
