Hi Mike, you could try

building 3–5 internal links from your strongest pages

Internal links are free and powerful.

Link from your best-performing pages to the target page using partial-match anchors:

Examples:

- “our furnace repair page.”
- “See heating services in \[location\]”

Add 2–3 local backlinks (the cheapest way to hit page 1)

You do not need expensive SEO backlinks.

These sources work extremely well and are nearly free:

- Local business directories (not generic ones—the local ones matter)
- Sponsorship links (sports teams, kids leagues, festivals)
- Local blogs or small media sites
- Supplier pages (“Installer Network,” “Certified Partner” listings)

Just 2–3 relevant local backlinks can push a page 2 site right onto page 1.
