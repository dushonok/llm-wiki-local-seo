Day 37 of My 90-Day Challenge: A Wrong Sitemap Found on a Live Site

Hi everyone,

Today I found a real mistake on SITE-06, my Longueuil French-drain site, which is already online but still set to noindex.

THE PROBLEM

The sitemap listed 46 URLs on the wrong domain extension: .ca, a domain that belongs to someone else, instead of .com. The pages also had no canonical tags. Google cannot index the site yet, so nothing was lost. But if I had opened it to indexing like this, I would have started on the wrong foot.

The cause is simple: my checks never verified the host name. That check now exists, and the fix is in progress.

WHAT ELSE MOVED TODAY

Thanks to @Brandon M's advice, the FAQ answers on the [Longueuil](https://drainfrancaislongueuil.com/questions-drain-francais-longueuil/ "https://drainfrancaislongueuil.com/questions-drain-francais-longueuil/") site are now flat on the page instead of hidden in accordions. That version is live. I also validated the legal pages for the site, and I reduced my call-tracking number from $60 to $30 a month. I keep it until my review on October 23.

WHERE THE DATA STANDS

Sherbrooke shows 0 clicks and 85 impressions in my last Search Console pull (Sept 26). [Longueuil](https://drainfrancaislongueuil.com/questions-drain-francais-longueuil/ "https://drainfrancaislongueuil.com/questions-drain-francais-longueuil/") has no organic visit yet, and I have no lead and no revenue at day 37.

THE LESSON

A site can look clean and still be wrong in a place nobody looks. Before any site goes live, the host, the sitemap and the canonicals must be checked.
