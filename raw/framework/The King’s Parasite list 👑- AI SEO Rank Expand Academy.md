i’m a fan of diversification and speed. For most of my stuff i have a solid budget to getter done so i find several vendors that do what they do well and i hire them to do what they do for each platform / type.

In some cases, one guy can build nearly all of a single type or sveral from several types.

These days im heavy on brand/product/naked URL anchors. Sometimes the product names have keywords in them.

I also have them embed a relevant YouTube video in the articles. Hard for Google to dismiss highly relevant content with their platform’s video also embeded.

I also diversify the content types even within the same parasite type: listicles, customer story, press style, vs, etc etc.

Run through it on one of your key properties and you’ll build a flow state.

I always provide vendors with a Writer’s Brief for deep context with keywords, entities, product/service page URL(s) etc so there is no confusion.
