Let me explain - I built [launchwithbryan.com](http://launchwithbryan.com "http://launchwithbryan.com") because I figured if I do this for clients I should do it for myself first.

Incorporated a real local business here in New Jersey. $160.

Connected it to a Google Business Profile — verified, still filling out the service areas and categories.

50 manual citations through a vetted Fiverr seller. $25.

So the whole entity foundation ran me under $200.

Indexation

Submitted through Omega Indexer. Sitting at 400+ pages and Omega reports them indexed — I'm still verifying that against GSC rather than taking the tool's word for it. Screenshots below.

The funnel — three steps, maybe a fourth for direct mail.

Step 1 is the free website offer. Short form, opts them in, tags them as interested.

Step 2 introduces Google Business Profile management and why it matters. Tags on interest.

Step 3 is a VSL for the CRM. Tags yes or no.

Every step tags, so by the time someone exits the funnel I know exactly what they raised their hand for.

I also have a YT video running right now as well see attached.

Where the real leverage is ⬇️

The email sequence behind it is 11 emails, each one built around a video. But the videos aren't embedded — they're thumbnails that click through to YouTube. That way the sequence feeds my channel while it nurtures the list.

Same asset doing two jobs.

And the list is the actual asset here. Most of these people say no to the front-end offer.

That's fine. The email sequence is retargeting — same logic as a Facebook pixel, except I own the channel and it doesn't cost me anything to run. Additional services, affiliate offers, whatever comes next, that audience is already qualified and already knows me.

The SEO services are the front door. The list is the business.

Happy to answer anything on the setup etc...
