## ✅ Exact-Match Domains:.com vs Everything Else

A real debate with multiple data points:

### ▶ For Local Service Micro-Sites

.com performs best

- +10–15% conversion lift (North America + Canada)
- Users are getting smart about “odd” domain suffixes

### ▶ For National / CPA / Traffic Arbitrage Models

TLD does *not* matter.

- Indexing speed > brand trust
- Affiliate payouts 100% unaffected by TLD
- Several EMDs on alternative TLDs indexed in 48 hours

Consensus:

- Local SEO: prioritize `.com`
- CPA / arbitrage: any EMD that can rank fast is fine

## ✅ Microsite Architecture: Critical Clarifications

### 1\. One Primary Service × Multiple Locations

Members asked whether to build 100+ “service × location” pages.

Shawn’s rule:  
Pick ONE main service → make location pages → other services = supporting pages.  
If another service is high-value, build a separate microsite.

### 2\. County → City Slug Structures

Some confusion around counties showing in site maps.

- County-first slugs are fine
- Must ensure every city actually generates a page
- Important: verify all locations appear in sitemap

### 3\. Do 5 Microsites, Not 1

Not all sites will “hit.”  
You dramatically increase your odds with five.

## ✅ AI Phone vs SMS Behavior (Local Market Differences)

Interesting discrepancy:

## ✅ GMB (Google Business Profiles) for Microsites

Heated discussion on whether to attempt GMB verification for micro-sites.

Key Points:

- Some members have successfully verified “lead gen” profiles.
- Using third-party office suites can work *if* suite numbers are adjusted.
- Google may request video verification → you can simply not complete it.
- No evidence (yet) that failed GMB attempts hurt organic rankings.
