# Groupe Nova — engagement notes and owner's estimates sheet (2026-10-19)

Captured from the owner's messages and spreadsheet screenshots during the
2026-10-19 session. Nothing here comes from the client directly; it is the owner's
(the person running this engagement) own statements and numbers. Recorded
verbatim or close to it so the wiki can cite it.

## Scope of the ongoing monthly support (owner's request)

- Optimization of service pages.
- Content creation in French and English, including local landing pages and blog
  articles.
- Listings in quality directories in Quebec and Canada.
- Ongoing Google Business Profile management.
- Monthly reporting covering rankings, calls and quote requests generated through
  Google, as well as work completed.
- Owner's personal preference: replace CTA buttons with embedded forms on the pages.
- Added later: optimize the homepage for Montreal.

## Pricing proposed by the owner

- Plans considered: $500 and $300 per month.
- Then: $500/month for the first 3 months to fix leftover issues, then $300/month
  ongoing.
- Latest proposal: **$600/month for the first 3 months**, then $300/month ongoing.
- Hourly rate implied by the owner's estimates sheet: $35/h.

## Google Ads (client plan, as relayed by the owner)

- The client wants to launch a Google Ads campaign in **February–March 2027**.
- Ad landing pages must be **separate from the normal website pages**.
- Owner's working assumption: one landing page per region.
- Service areas listed by the owner: Montreal, Montreal East, West Island, North
  Shore, South Shore and Lanaudière. This matches the website's own footer line
  ("Zones desservies: Montréal, Montréal-Est, West Island, Rive-Nord, Rive-Sud et
  Lanaudière", see site-crawl-and-gbp-check-2026-10-19.md) and differs from the
  original engagement brief (Montreal, Laval, North Shore, South Shore,
  Montérégie, Lanaudière).
- The owner wants this region question added to the list of questions for the
  client (not yet sent or answered).
